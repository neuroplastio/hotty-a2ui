# 2026-10-23.2 — KIT-WEIGHT: a Row's weights as a host shares them

plx's settings page: "the same Row lays out differently in the two". In
cells each child of a Row started at its natural width and the weights
shared only the spare; on a host a weighted child is `flex: weight 1 0%`
(A2UI's own renderer has `flex: weight`), so it starts from nothing and
the weights share everything the unweighted children leave.

**Now cells shares them as a host does** (share): an unweighted child at
its natural width, a weighted one from its minimum; the columns left go
to the weighted by weight, whatever they hold. A child whose share is
narrower than its minimum (its longest word) is held there and the others
share the rest, as a flex row's min-content does. Justify stretch weighs
every child, 1 when unweighted, and now keeps those minimums too.

So Rows weighted alike line up as columns. The icons grid shows it: four
icons a row, weight 1 each, once as wide as each name and now a grid.
The kanban's three columns are equal; keycaps' option rows move a column
or two. Rows that weigh only one child (a label beside a control) look
as they did.

**Checked:** the gate; TestRowWeights (halves of unequal texts, weights
beside a control, one held at its minimum); the stories with weighted
Rows drawn before and after (icons, keycaps, drag-kanban, qr, tree,
slider).

**For the maintainer's look:** the icons grid and the kanban in cells.
