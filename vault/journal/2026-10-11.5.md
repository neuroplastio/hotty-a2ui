# 2026-10-11.5 — Round 8: a page is the surface, not a box's content

Round 8 ([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-11-review-round-8.md)) kept the
confirm's blank row and the title's shadow (journal 2026-10-11.4). On
the markdown page it went further than round 7: the plain scroll view
"just removed the background of the bounding box"; the box itself is
the problem, the page "hidden inside <textarea> instead of just being on
a page".

So a docs page is the surface's content, in no HottyScrollView, and the
surface scrolls as a whole, as a web page does:

- **On a host** nothing changes: the surface is placed with `scroll`
  and the host scrolls it (SPEC §5.3); a heading a link goes to takes
  the focus, which scrolls it into view.
- **In cells** the frame is the whole page, and the program that shows
  it scrolls it, as a host would. The storybook's pane and `storybook
  -bare` now share `storybook.Scroll`: the wheel, Page Down and Space,
  Page Up and Shift+Space when the element with the keyboard leaves
  them, the element with the keyboard kept in sight, and a jump to a
  heading of a page's HottyMarkdown (`OnPage`: no HottyScrollView holds
  it) puts the heading at the top (`State.Jump`). `-bare` scrolled
  nothing before: a story taller than the screen was cut at its bottom.
- **The story** `hotty/markdown` has no `box`; the caption and the help
  line come after the page, as a page's footer.
- **`variant: "plain"` is gone** (it was 2c49d14's): it advised exactly
  what the maintainer rejected. Catalog instruction 11 now says not to
  put a page in a HottyScrollView, and 27 to put it in the surface
  itself. Profile §6.27 "The page scrolls as a whole", §6.11.

Tests: TestPagePane (the Book: the jump at the top, Space, Shift+Space,
Page Up, Page Down) and TestBarePage (`-bare`: the screen's rows, the
jump, Space). The vector that went through the links starts on the first
link, since there is no box to start from.
