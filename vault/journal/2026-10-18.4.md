# 2026-10-18.4 — a List's row whose label is a Row

plexos' agents list needs each agent's row to hold a dot and a word in
its status's tone, the name taking the room between them, and the whole
row to go to the agent on a click or Enter: a menu's row with a mark and
a word at its end. A vertical List's Button was such a row already
(`Item`), but cells drew its label as one line of plain text in the
button's style, so its tones and its layout went.

What changed:
- **cells**: an item Button whose label is a Row or a Column
  (`richItem`) is that, laid out across the row inside a column of
  padding each side, in its own colours and attributes (`paintRich`).
  The whole row takes the click; with the keyboard it is reversed in
  `accent` end to end, as a plain row is, and a disabled one is faint
  and muted. Its natural and minimum widths are its label's and 2; its
  height is its label's.
- **html**: a `k-item`'s Row or Column label fills it, so a weighted
  child pushes what follows it to the row's end.
- `docs/profile.md`: the Button rows of §1's and §3.4's tables, and the
  widths table.
- Test: `rendition/cells/richrow_test.go`.

Also, the commit before: a Text's backslash escapes and entities read in
cells as on a host (`view.textOf`, `TestTextEscapes`).
