# 2026-10-10.30 — KIT-19c: HottyBigText

The phase-1 leg of KIT-19: text in large letters, as OpenTUI's ASCIIFont
draws it. Story `hotty/bigtext`: a title screen (HOTTY, large and centred
over a caption), a score bound to the data model, the three sizes of one
line, and a French line too long for one, wrapped between words and
centred, its accents left off.

**The reference.** Bubble Tea has no big text, so it is OpenTUI's
ASCIIFont (`ref/opentui/bigtext.ts`, `make shot NAME=bigtext` with
`KIT_KEYS=`, `ROWS=40`), set with the story's content in its `tiny` (2
rows) and `block` (6 rows, with a box-drawing shadow) fonts. ASCIIFont
does not wrap: its `block` line is cut at the terminal's edge. Its fonts
are cfonts' (`homepage` in OpenTUI's bundle), which are GPL-3.0, so the
kit takes nothing from them.

**The catalog** (profile §6.28, instruction 28, as the coordinator
assigned: 27 is another worker's leg). `HottyBigText` takes `text` (a
DynamicString, required), `size` (`small`, `medium`, the default, or
`large`), `align` (`start`, `center`, `end`) and `weight`.
- `size`, not the gap analysis's sketched `font`: the three differ in
  how large they are, which is what an agent picks between, and on a host
  they are one face at three sizes. A style of letters (outlined, shaded,
  ASCIIFont's `shade` or `slick`) would be a prop of its own, if wanted.
- `align` on the component, though a Column's `align` places a child: a
  banner's lines each go across its room, wrapped or not.
- No colour prop: it is `fg`, as a heading is (L7).

**The fonts are the kit's own**, drawn pixel by pixel for this leg
(`rendition/cells/bigfont.go`); no font file's data is copied, so there
is no licence to carry beyond the repository's. Each has the capitals, the
digits (as wide as each other, so that a score or a time stays put) and
`!"#$%&'()*+,-./:;<=>?@[\]_`.
- Medium: 5×7 pixels, a dot-matrix display's letter, in half blocks, a
  pixel a column and half a row, about square in a cell: 4 rows, the last
  half row blank.
- Small: 3×5, in half blocks: 3 rows. The round letters (G, O, Q) and N
  are 4 wide, M and W 5: at 3 they blurred into H and each other.
- Large: the small font's glyphs, a pixel two columns and a row of `█`: 5
  rows.

Block elements (`▀ ▄ █`) rather than figlet's ASCII: terminals draw them
themselves (kitty, ghostty, wezterm, foot, xterm.js, hottyterm), so the
pixels meet without a seam, and they read as letters, not letters made of
letters. Quadrants were tried in thought and left: a quadrant's pixel is
half a column and half a row, twice as tall as wide, which makes 3 to 5
rows of letters narrow and spindly; half blocks give square pixels.

**Cells** (`rendition/cells/bigtext.go`): letters a column apart, a word
a space's glyph (3 pixels medium, 2 small and large) and a column each
side, lines a blank row apart and a blank row after it (the separator
rule), so that its letters do not meet what follows. A line too long
wraps at its last space that fits; a word too long for a line of its own
breaks between letters, so nothing is cut. Natural width: its widest line
unwrapped; minimum: its widest word. Lowercase draws in capitals, a Latin
letter's mark is left off (`É` is `E`, a table of Latin-1 and Latin
Extended-A, since x/text's norm would bring x/text back into every
program, KIT-27), and anything else is `?`.
```
█▀▀▀▄ █▀▀▀▀ ▄▀▀▀▄ █▀▀▄  █   █ ▄▀▀▀▄     ▄▀▀▀▄ ▄▀▀▀▄ █
█▄▄▄▀ █▄▄▄  █▄▄▄█ █   █  ▀▄▀     ▄▀     █ ▄▄▄ █   █ █
█ ▀▄  █     █   █ █  ▄▀   █     ▀       █   █ █   █ ▀
▀   ▀ ▀▀▀▀▀ ▀   ▀ ▀▀▀     ▀     ▀        ▀▀▀▀  ▀▀▀  ▀
```

**HTML:** a `div.k-big` holding its text as it is, its lines apart at a
`br`: display type, weight 800, tabular figures, `font-size` 3, 4 or 5
rows (`--hotty-cell-h`) by its size and `line-height` one row more, which
leaves room for descenders that line-height 1 let into the next line.
`k-big-center`, `k-big-end`. A line too long wraps as text does. The
host's own look (display type proper) is KIT-19h.

**Text:** its text as it is, a line each of its lines, case and accents
kept: `Ready? Go!`.

**Checked:** `make check`; view, cells, html and text tests (every font
has every character, rows even, digits one width; the three sizes drawn
exactly; lowercase, an accent and an unknown character; wrapping at a
space, breaking a word, align, a Row's natural width; no rows without
text; the HTML's classes and br, and a bound text's delta); catalog
validation cases. Pictures, each looked at:
- `make shot NAME=bigtext`: OpenTUI beside the kit, Catppuccin Mocha.
- The story in Catppuccin Latte (VHS).
- A specimen of every glyph at all three sizes, dark and light, from
  which the small font's G, O, Q, N, M, W and K were redrawn.
- `hotty render` at 1.6x of the story's HTML, terminal and material
  light.

**Open for the maintainer:**
- `size` rather than `font` (above), and no style choice yet.
- Cells shows capitals only; the host shows the text's own case, since
  display type has lowercase. Upper-casing the host too would make the
  renditions agree, at the cost of the text as written.
- The large size reuses the small font doubled; a finer 5-row font (say
  7×10 pixels in half blocks) would be smoother, at the cost of a third
  glyph set.
- A blank row after it, always (the separator rule): a banner over a form
  is set apart, but two big texts in a row stack a row apart too.
