# 2026-10-27.1 — A plx tool author's reports: Rows of menus, Text on one line

hotty relayed three reports from plx's feedback inbox: one tool author's
notes from building a plx tool with A2UI, seen in hottyterm and in
Chrome against storybook -page at 51ecdcd. They're data, filed on the
board with their Source lines. The Blitz halves are hotty's.

- **KIT-ROW-MENUS (75eed68).** Their Row held three Buttons and two
  menus, each a one-item template of a Modal with a Button trigger. The
  templates are Rows, and `wraps` counted a Row as a column of cards, so
  the Row wasn't k-wrap: in Chrome its Buttons shrank until each label
  broke, icon over text. `inline` now counts a Modal whose trigger is
  inline, and a Row of only inline things. In Blitz the menus drop to a
  second line, whole. TestRowWrapsMenus fails on the old rule.
- **KIT-TEXT-LINES.** `io_neuroplast_hotty.lines` on a Text, a count, not
  a boolean, so that a summary can keep two rows: the first `lines`, the
  last cut with …. One line is the first line unwrapped and cut at its
  room, as text-overflow cuts it, not the first wrapped row with … after
  it, which would leave the row's end blank. A clamped Text's minimum is
  a column, so in a Row the weighted title gives way and the badge beside
  it stays whole. The view field is `Clamp`, since Element.Lines is a
  scroll view's. In cells the … has no space before it.
  - On a host, 1 is `k-one-line`: nowrap, hidden overflow, ellipsis, the
    first block alone. More is `k-clamp`, `--k-clamp`, -webkit-line-clamp
    and line-clamp. Chrome clamps the story's note to two rows with …;
    Blitz cuts the one line (hotty-blitz 0.5.3) but has no line-clamp, so
    hottyterm shows the note whole. Told hotty, no kit workaround.
  - Story hotty/lines: four tasks, titles on a line beside due dates, and
    a note of two lines, seen at 60 and 36 columns in cells, in Blitz and
    in Chromium.
