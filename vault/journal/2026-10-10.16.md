# 2026-10-10.16 — Round 2 review: filled Buttons in cells

One of the maintainer's round 2 notes
([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-10-review-round-2.md)), on the validator:
"The button with square brackets though I would change (I saw nice
square buttons in round 1)". Round 1's were huh's Confirm, Yes and No,
filled boxes. So a Button in cells is ` label ` on a fill now, not
`[ label ]`. The html rendition already fills its Buttons; the text
rendition keeps `[ label ]`, as a pipe has no background.

**The face.** A column of padding each side, filled, so its natural
width is the label + 2 (it was + 4), the same as a List's row, which it
was already. Borderless stays its label, underlined, unfilled; a List's
Buttons, its rows, stay as they were, unfilled.

**Each state**, chosen by looking at the storybook in VHS (Catppuccin
Mocha, the terminal's colours learned) beside round 2's:
- **Unfocused**: a tint 48/255 from `bg` toward `fg` (#3f4053 on
  Mocha's #1e1e2e), a grey a step or two off the background, as huh's
  is. Where the background can't be tinted but colours show (the floor,
  or before the terminal answers), SGR 100, the terminal's bright black
  as the background under its own text, which is huh's grey there.
  That's a new kind of fallback in `Cell`: `BackShade`, beside
  `BackAttr` and `MonoAttr`.
- **Focused**: reversed in `accent`, unfilled, as before; the padding
  goes reversed too, so the focus is the same box in the accent.
- **Primary**: bold, nothing else. `accent` stays focus's (§3.6), and a
  stronger fill read as focus at a glance.
- **Disabled**: first `muted` and faint on the fill, as before. On the
  fill it almost vanished, and at the floor it would: `muted` is bright
  black, and so is the fill there. Now its label is `fg`, faint, on half
  the fill (24/255). Both halves say disabled: a dimmer label and a
  shallower box. A full fill with the faint label was tried beside it;
  that read as an enabled button with grey text.
- **NO_COLOR**: underlined, padding too, where nothing fills. Reverse is
  focus's, so it can't be the fill. Brackets were the other way. They'd
  make a Button 2 columns wider under NO_COLOR than in colour. The layout
  is the same in every output mode, since the frame's text is fixed when
  it's painted. Seen in VHS: the underlines read as controls, and a Row's
  column between two Buttons, not underlined, keeps them apart. A
  disabled one is faint and underlined.
