# 2026-10-23.10 — KIT-LARGE: a large Button for a call to action

The maintainer, through plx-settings, on plx's onboarding (plx 807e2e3a,
kit 23f95a9) on a hotty host: "Buttons are too thin". A slide is a
kicker, a HottyBigText title, a lead, chips, and a Row of Back and Skip
(borderless) and Continue (primary). plx sets no CSS of its own. Through
`hotty render` a replica's Continue was 2.25rem, a field's height, a
little taller than the chips beside it, and Back and Skip a row (a
borderless Button has no min-height): slight under a big title.

**`io_neuroplast_hotty.size`** on a basic Button: `large` or `medium`,
the default, as it was. Not a taller default: a medium Button is as tall
as a field, which a form, a dialog and a card want (Save beside its
field). An extension, since Button is basic's and A2UI v1.0 gives it a
variant and no size.

**On a host,** `k-large`: at least 3rem tall, 0.625rem over and under
its label and 1.5rem each side; an icon one at least 3rem wide, round.
Every variant takes it, so a borderless large Button is as tall as a
filled one and a Back/Skip/Continue row lines up. A vertical List's row
keeps its row's size. The label's font is the surface's.

**In cells** a large Button is a medium one, ` label ` on a row: a row
is the least a terminal draws, and its fill already sets it apart. The
text rendition shows the label.

**Story:** hotty/size: a slide's Back, Skip and Continue, large, over the
same three medium and a Save beside a field. Behaviours sort by title,
so storybook's TestNav takes one more step past it to the Drag and drop
topic.

**Checked:** the gate; hotty/size and the replica through `hotty render`
at 90×26 (Continue 81px at 1.6x where medium was 60, Back and Skip as
tall) and headless Chromium (48px against 36).

Profile §6.38 and §2's Button row; catalog instruction 39 and the
`size` property.
