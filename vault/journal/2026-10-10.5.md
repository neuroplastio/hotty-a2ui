# 2026-10-10.5 — KIT-22c: HottySwitch

A phase-1 leg the maintainer added (2026-10-10): an iPhone-like on/off
switch for a boolean. Story `hotty/switch`: a phone's settings (airplane
mode, Wi-Fi, a hotspot that airplane mode disables, a switch that is on
and disabled, a label-less one beside a Text) and a HottyForm with a
field, a switch with a check and a hint line.

**The reference.** Bubble Tea, bubbles and lipgloss have no switch, and
neither has OpenTUI. The closest is huh's Confirm, Charm's field for a
boolean, set `Inline` with "On" and "Off" as its buttons
(`ref/gaps.go`, `make shot NAME=switch` with `REF_KEYS=Tab`, `ROWS=30`,
`COLS=64`). It is held against for the field's frame (the title, the
focus bar, the error, the hints), not for the glyph. Where they differ:
- Confirm is two buttons, the chosen one filled; a switch is one control
  whose knob's end is the state.
- Confirm moves with ←/→ (h/l) and takes y/n, and Enter goes on to the
  next field; a switch flips on Space and Enter, as the kit's CheckBox
  and Button do.
- huh puts a blank row between fields; a stack of title-less switches
  stays tight, as a settings list is (the separator rule).
- huh has no disabled field, so the reference can't show one.

**The catalog** (profile §6.17, instruction 17): `HottySwitch` has
`label`, `value` (DynamicBoolean, required), `disabled`, `checks` and
`weight`. Instruction 17 says to prefer it to a CheckBox for a setting
that takes effect at once, as Apple's and Material's guidance has it,
and to keep the CheckBox for a choice a form sends later.

**An L1 effect, found by the story:** a hotty component's dynamic props
and checks can't call basic's functions, since a catalog can't import
another. The first story had `disabled: not(/airplane)` and a check
with `or`; both failed validation. So `disabled` and checks' conditions
are literals or paths (vault a2ui-limits L1, profile §6.17). KIT-CAT's
composite catalog is the fix.

**The view** (`view/switch.go`): Kind `Switch`, with `Label`, `Value` (a
bool), `Disabled` and `Error`. It takes focus unless disabled. Activate
flips it, and does nothing while disabled.

**Cells:** a field (gutter, error row) whose face is a three-column
track and a knob, then the label:
- on: `━━●`, heavy, in `accent`;
- off: `●──`, light, in `muted`;
- focused: the field's `┃` bar, and the label in `accent`;
- disabled: all faint and `muted`; on and disabled keeps `━━●` but
  muted.

The knob's end and the heavy or light track say the state without
colour, so it reads in a monochrome terminal. Candidates drawn in
Catppuccin Mocha and Latte and dropped: reverse-video pills (a block, and
heavy beside text), `(●  )` in parentheses, half circles `◖◗` (gaps in
Meslo), and a track four or more wide (read as a slider).

**HTML:** a `button type=button role=switch aria-checked`, holding a
pill track with a knob, then the label. The off state is Material 3's:
a tonal track with a `muted` ring and a `muted` knob, which reads on
light and dark themes; on is an `accent` track with an `on-accent` knob
at the far end. The ring and the focus ring are box-shadows, since
Blitz drops thin rounded borders at fractional scales. The knob jumps:
Blitz runs no CSS transitions, so its slide, by deltas, is KIT-22h.

**Keys:** Space, Enter or a click flips it. On a host it is a button,
which the host clicks on Space and Enter (SPEC §10.2), so Enter flips it
in a HottyForm too, as it presses a Button there, rather than submitting
the form as Enter on a CheckBox does. Cells does the same, so the two
renditions play the same vector. Enter in the form's text field still
submits. huh's way, where Enter goes on, would need the switch's Enter
bound to `program` on a host: the maintainer's call, if wanted.

**Text:** `Label: on` or `off`, `(disabled)` after a disabled one, and
the error line under it.

**Checked:** `make check`; the vector in both renditions (Space and
Enter flip; Tab skips a disabled one, also once a path disables it; a
click flips, and a click on a disabled one does nothing; Enter flips in
the form, and Enter in the field is held by the check, then submits
with the switches' values); the story by `hotty render` at 1.6x in
terminal and material light, focused and with an error; the shot, and
one with the check's error; the storybook in a pty, the story under
Components.

## After the maintainer's look

The maintainer found `━━●` too much like a short slider and asked for a
boxy switch in cells. I drew candidates in their terminal (a pane
printing them as cells, plus a vhs capture), each in a stack of
settings, as a settings list puts switches on consecutive rows, and each
without colour too:
- **Filled boxes** (reverse-video pills, `ON`/`OFF` boxes, a box with a
  knob and a word) merge into one column when neighbouring rows are both
  on, because a cell's background fills its whole height.
- **Full-height blocks** (`█`, `▐█`, shades) merge the same way.
- **Two-tone bars** in ⅞ and ¾ blocks keep a gap between rows, but
  without colour on and off are the same block.
- **Brackets with a knob** stay apart and read without colour. The
  maintainer picked these, then asked for different blocks on and off,
  then for fewer cells.

**Picked:** `▬▬■` on, in the accent: a bar into a filled knob. `□──`
off, in muted: a hollow knob on a thin line. Both are three columns,
frameless, as the old face was, so widths and layout don't change. The
knob's end, its fill and the track's weight say the state without
colour. Dropped on the way: the same in brackets (`[▬▬■]`, 5 columns;
`[▬■]`, 4), in parentheses (`(▬■)`), with a thin frame (`▕▬■▏`, whose
eighths join across rows), and frameless in 4 (`▬▬▬■`). The host's pill
stays as it was.

**The slider follows.** The maintainer saw the circle sit off the line
in their terminal, the switch's old `━━●` and the slider's `━━━━●────`
alike, and asked for the slider's knob to be a square too. A Slider in
cells is `━━━━■────` now: `■` sits on the line's middle. While the
slider has the keyboard the knob is in the accent with the track up to
it, not reversed, since a reversed square is a dark hole in an accent
cell. Drawn and dropped: `▬` for the done part (a long track breaks
into dashes) and `█` as the knob (a tall, heavy handle).
