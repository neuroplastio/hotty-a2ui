# 2026-10-13.3 — Drags on a host, before G1 and G2

The maintainer: "let's fix drag and drop in hotty mode. Currently, it
selects the text instead of dragging in all storybook examples". The host
had no drags at all: KIT-23h waited on G1 and G2, and nothing opted in to
§9.1, so the host selected text.

**What landed** (`rendition/html/drag.go`; profile §6.21 "Where"):
- What can be dragged has `drag` in its `data-on`: a HottyList's item, a
  HottyTable's row, a HottyTree's node, a List's child, a drag source.
- The gesture is cells'. The press arms the drag, and the item lifts once
  the host names another element or none, so a press and a release stay
  a click. Lifting on the press would select the item first, and the
  click would then act on it.
- Without G1 the item takes the place of the one it is over, and goes
  into a node that takes children: the view's rule without pixels.
- A drag that came back where it began ends with the host's click there,
  which the rendition swallows. Escape cancels.
- The look: the lifted item is faint, and a line in the accent shows
  where it would land, in the gap between items. A folder it would go
  into is reversed in the accent. Checked with `hotty render` on all four
  stories. The line is a pseudo-element, because Blitz draws inset and
  offset shadows with hairline frames (table cells, the kanban's
  buttons).

**Tests:** `rendition/html/drag_test.go` covers the four stories on
hottytest. It checks a move, a click, a drag that comes back, Escape,
into a folder, a table row, and a card between columns.

**Still blocked:**
- G2: S4's empty column, and S5's assignees (drop targets that aren't
  drag sources). The host can't name them, and a kit-side `drag` on them
  would be the workaround the maintainer ruled out.
- G1: halves and thirds, and beside a folder.
- Grips for touch: until they land, a touch scrolls.
