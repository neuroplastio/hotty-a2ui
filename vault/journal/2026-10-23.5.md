# 2026-10-23.5 — KIT-PLAIN: a scroll view with no box

plx's settings page scrolls its sections between a head and its buttons,
which stay. The only HottyScrollView the kit had was a box, filled and
round-cornered with padding inside, and it "always looks like a log
box".

**`variant: "plain"`.** HottyScrollView is the kit's own component, so
the look is a prop of its own, with basic's word for a component's look
(Button's `variant`). `"box"`, the default, is what was; `"plain"` is
the body of a screen: no fill, corners or padding, its content where
what is above it starts, and no ring. A screen that scrolls as a whole
still needs none: the surface scrolls (profile §6.27, round 8).

**Its focus is on its scrollbar.** On a host the thumb is in the focus
colour (`scrollbar-color`), but an overlay scrollbar, Blitz's, shows no
thumb until the box scrolls, and none where its content fits. So a
focused plain box also has a 2px line in the focus colour down its
right edge, an inset shadow, whatever the thumb does. A gap of `--k-2`
before the edge keeps the content off it. In cells the thumb is accent
while the box has the keyboard, as it was. Where all of the content
shows, the bar was not drawn, so a focused box showed nothing in cells.
Now the thumb is the whole bar there, in accent, for a box as well as a
plain one.

**Story:** hotty/stretch's sections are plain.

**Checked:** the gate; cells at 60×22; hotty/stretch through
`hotty render` at 70×24, as is and with the focus rule applied (a 2px
line down the box's right edge).

**For the maintainer's look:** hotty/stretch on a host and in cells,
with the keyboard on the sections and without. Unfocused on a host with
an overlay scrollbar, only the cut at the body's foot says that it
scrolls. Rules above and below it would say so, at the cost of some of
a box's look.
