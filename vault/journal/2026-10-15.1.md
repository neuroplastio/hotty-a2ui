# 2026-10-15.1 — A click on the nav keeps its place; a list's column takes its drags

The maintainer found two kit bugs in the storybook; hotty relayed them
with a HOTTY log of the first, and the maintainer said to fix both.

**The nav jumps on a click after a trackpad scroll, and the wrong story
opens** (c576ab3). A press on a HottyTree's box focuses the box (SPEC
§10.1: a click focuses nothing without a tabindex of 0 or more, so a
node's row takes none). `Event` recorded the host's focus as the box,
and `Update` then sent focus to the selected node (`focusOn`), which the
host scrolled into view before the release: the click landed on the row
then under the pointer. Now a focus the user gives is taken as it is:
`r.host.focus` is what `focusOn` would send, so nothing goes out until
the selection moves (the click's own selection, a key). The same holds
for a diff's hunk, a file picker's entry and a document's heading. The
cost: Tab into a box whose selection is out of sight scrolls to it at
the first key that moves it, not at once. hotty's other way, placing
such surfaces with `p=1` to tell a click's focus from Tab's by the
`press` before it, needs every program to place so; not taken.
TestTreeClickKeepsItsPlace fails without the fix.

**Kanban: "If I empty the column, I can't move anything else into it.
And the drop zone is very small."** Live on hottyterm 26.10.14
(f81c379), on the private headless sway:
- On a host, an emptied List kept its 2.5rem box (`kit.css`
  `:empty`) and took a drop there; anywhere else in the column, nothing
  did.
- In cells, an emptied List had no rows at all, and nothing took a drop.

Now (view, both renditions):
- `DropTarget`: where nothing under the pointer takes the drag, the
  nearest Stack holding exactly one list whose items would move takes it
  for that list (`view.ListOf`), its item past the list's last.
- `overMove`: past the last item is after it.
- HTML: such a container has `drop` in its `data-on`, so a host with
  `drop` names it over the heading and the room under the cards.
- Cells: an empty List whose items move keeps one row (`measureHeight`),
  as the host keeps a card's height, so the line shows.

Over the heading, a card lands after the last on a host and before the
first in cells (`nearest`).

**Checked:** the gate. TestDragKanbanColumn (cells: under Done's last
card, into the emptied Doing, over Doing's heading) and
TestDragKanbanColumnOnHost (a `drop` host: over To do's heading, into
the emptied Doing) both fail without the fix.

**Not changed:** in cells, an emptied column narrows to its heading's
width. A weighted Row child there starts at its natural width and takes
spare columns by weight (profile, Row widths), where a host gives
weighted columns equal shares (`flex: w 1 0%`). Told the maintainer.

**Later, the line at a List's ends:** "drop zone in HOTTY mode lacks any
indication when dropping into the column with one item in it". The kit
marked the last card `k-drop-after`, but its line is drawn just below
the card, and the List's own box (`k-overflow`, `overflow: auto`)
clipped it; the line before a first card the same. Now the line at a
List's ends goes just inside its first or last item (`kit.css`, as a
tree's node has it). Checked live on hottyterm 26.10.14 (f81c379): over
Done's room under its one card, the line along the card's bottom; over
its top half, along its top; the drop lands there.
