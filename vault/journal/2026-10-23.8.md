# 2026-10-23.8 — KIT-NAV: a compact HottyList for a nav

plx's settings page: "HottyList is heavy for a nav sidebar". Bubbles'
list gives each item its label over its description, a blank row between
items that have one, and a status line and a blank row under the title,
so six pages took ten rows (twenty-one with descriptions), four of them
the head's.

**HottyList `variant`:** `list`, the default, as it was, or `compact`.
A compact list has an item a row, its description two columns after its
label in muted; its title bold in muted, a sidebar's heading, with no
blank row under it (its filter, while typed, takes the same row); no
status line; and, while it is paged, its dots on the row under its
items. Six pages and a title are seven rows. The selection is the list's
bar, muted with a bold label, the accent while it has the keyboard, and
the arrows, the pages, the filter, the moves and the clicks are the
list's, so only the look changes. The text rendition draws it as a list.

A variant, not an extension, as HottyScrollView's `plain`: HottyList is
the kit's own component, so its look is its own prop.

**Where it does not fit,** the description goes first: in cells the
label and the description are cut together with `…`, which cuts the
description; on a host each is cut with an ellipsis and the description
shrinks a thousand times as fast (`flex-shrink: 1000`). `flex: 1 1 0`
would cut it first too, but leaves it out of the list's natural width,
so a sidebar sized to its content cut every description (seen through
Blitz). Titles and dots start past the bar's 3px, where labels do, as in
cells.

**Separators:** a compact list has a title row only when it has a title,
so two titled ones in a Column have a blank row between them, a
sidebar's sections; two untitled ones run together, as one list.

**Story:** hotty/nav: a sidebar of two compact lists, Settings' six pages
and three recent workspaces with their paths beside them, beside a page
whose head follows the selections.

**Checked:** the gate; hotty/nav in cells at 72 and 40 columns; through
`hotty render` at 72×22, and narrowed to 8.5rem, where the paths are cut
and the labels whole (Blitz clips where a browser draws the ellipsis).
