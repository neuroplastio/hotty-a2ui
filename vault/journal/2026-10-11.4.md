# 2026-10-11.4 — Round 7 review: clock, plain dots, a page unboxed, a shadow

Round 7 ([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-11-review-round-7.md)) looked at
the legs since round 6 beside their references, and at the look
questions they left. What changed:

- **"HTML over the TTY".** The big-text story's subtitle said "web pages
  in a terminal", which misdescribes HOTTY; the story and the OpenTUI
  reference say HTML over the TTY now.
- **Timers write a clock by default** (`4:59`, `0:09.5`); `format:
  "duration"` keeps Bubble Tea's `4m59s`. The catalog's instruction 25,
  profile §6.25 and §3.4; the stories' lap keeps duration.
- **A paginator's other dots are plain**, in `border`, no longer faint;
  under NO_COLOR they stay faint, as nothing else tells the page shown
  apart. `Cell.MonoAttr` now applies to any cell under NO_COLOR, not
  only a tinted one.
- **A blank row before a Button after a Text** (or a Row that starts
  with one), as huh sets a question's buttons apart. Profile §3.3's
  Column rule.
- **HottyScrollView `variant: "plain"`**: the page itself, no fill, no
  padding (cells) or `k-plain` (host); the scrollbar stays. The markdown
  story's page uses it, as glamour's layout has it, and keeps the kit's
  alerts, which the maintainer preferred to glamour's quote. Catalog
  instruction 11, profile §6.11, §2 and §3.4.
- **HottyBigText `shadow`**, for "I like the 3d effect though": the
  letters cast a shadow a column and half a row down and to the right,
  in `border`, and are a column further apart. Where ink and shadow share
  a cell, the ink's half block sits on the shadow's colour (SGR 100 where
  the terminal hasn't said its colours); under NO_COLOR the shadow is
  faint. A whole pixel at large (two columns by a row) swamped the
  letters in the first picture, so at large it is half one. On a host it
  is a `text-shadow`, which Blitz doesn't draw yet. The story's title
  has it, for round 8's A/B.

Kept as they are: the kit's big letters (more readable small), its
confirm-form (no highlight before you reach Yes), page mode's live
controls ("should look usable"), the kit's paginator.

New, low priority: a cue for the default button, Windows XP style, small
and symmetric, not an underline (board KIT-DEF).

The timer pair asked "do the times and their labels read well?", and the
maintainer couldn't tell what it was for. A pair needs a question about
one difference that the two sides show; a "does it read well" pair of
kit and reference is no question. Round 8's brief asks one per pair.
