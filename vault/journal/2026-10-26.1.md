# 2026-10-26.1 — Caps in the shifted case, and a gap between a sequence's

The maintainer, on caps in plx: `keys.style.case` should have a third
state, lower case unless Shift is held, the Shift hidden and the capital
showing it; and the `>` between a sequence's caps is a bit much, so an
option to leave just some space.

- **`Keycaps.Case` `shifted`.** Words are lower, as in `lower`, but a
  letter with Shift is its capital and the Shift goes: `ctrl+shift+p` is
  ctrl+P, `G` is G, on a Mac's glyphs ⌘P. Only letters fold. SPEC §10.4
  already writes any other character with Shift in it as the one it types
  (`?`), so a key with no capital keeps its Shift (shift+tab, shift+1).
  A letter the user relabels keeps its Shift too, since its label can't
  show the capital.
- **`Keycaps.Then`** stands between a sequence's caps, and
  `Keycaps.Between` gives it, `>` where it is unset. A blank one is a
  gap alone: on a host a `span.k-cap-gap`, empty, 0.4em wide; in cells a
  column untinted, between the caps' own tinted padding. The screen
  reader's words (`CapsText`) are as they were.
- **hotty/keycaps** has Shifted in Case and a Between cycle (> or Space)
  in the second row, now four: Glue glyphs is Glue, so the row fits at
  90 columns in cells (at 80 the first row was already cut).

Seen in Blitz at 1.6x (`hotty render`): [⌃+b] [%] with the gap, ⌃+P,
⇧+tab, meta+⌃+alt+R; the default unchanged, [⌃+B] > [%]. In cells,
`ctrl+b   %` and `ctrl+P`.

plx's side is plx's: `keys.style.case` offers auto, title and lower, and
there's no setting for Then. Told plx's agent what to add.
