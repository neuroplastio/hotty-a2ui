# 2026-10-10.19 — Round 3 review: a quieter underline, the error under the value

Round 3 won every pair ([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-10-review-round-3.md)).
Two new notes, from the caret pair, in cells (KIT-02c):

- "Can we tone down the underline a bit? The accent color, but less
  contrast. In both states, so it's there, but not in the way of the eyes
  when reading the text." A one-line field's underline is now the accent
  halfway to the background (`fieldLineMix`, 128/255; `Cell.LineMix`), the
  same with the keyboard or without it. The gutter's `┃` and the label in
  the accent say which field has the keyboard. Where the terminal hasn't
  said its colours, the line is in `border`, the floor's quiet one: the
  accent there is the terminal's full blue.
- "Validation error should get left padding, aligned with the input". A
  one-line field's error starts in its input's column (`errorIndent`),
  unless that leaves it fewer than 16 columns; then it starts at the
  field's edge, as before.

The strength is a guess at "a bit": round 4 shows it beside round 3, and
two other strengths, 96 and 160, side by side.
