# 2026-10-13.2 — KIT-DEF: the default button's cue

Round 7 asked for a cue on the button Enter presses, Windows XP style,
small, not an underline, not huh's picked look.

**Which button.** A HottyForm's default is the first Button inside whose
action is the form's onSubmit (`view.Element.Default`, marked in the
form's mapper). Instruction 24 already has a question's Yes carry the
form's event, so confirm-form's Yes is its default with no change to the
story, and Enter in the form does what pressing it does. No new prop.

**The look, as the maintainer steered it in the storybook:**
- Proposed: both padding columns' outer edges as bars (`▏Yes▕`), shown
  only while Enter would press it. Kept: the left bar alone, "Let's try
  keeping only the left line".
- Shown always, "even when No is focused / clicked", and "when Yes is
  focused as well". The rule for when Enter would press it went.
- While the default has the keyboard, it is reversed in the accent, and
  the reversed bar is the background's colour at its outer edge, lost in
  what is round it. It is `▎` in `fg` on the accent there. A dark bar inside
  the padding, by the label, was stronger but moved and crowded it.
- On a host: `k-enter` (`k-default` is the default variant's class), a
  3px line inside the left edge in `fg` at 55%, full strength on the
  focus colour. Blitz draws it at 1.6x.
