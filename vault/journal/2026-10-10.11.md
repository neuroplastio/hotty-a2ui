# 2026-10-10.11 — Round 1 review: the terminal's colours, diff tints

From the maintainer's round 1 review
([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-10-review-round-1.md)): the diff "need[s]
colored tint on modified lines (keeping syntax highlighting) and less
toxic background on the in-line diff"; the progress bar has "no
gradient"; the viewport wants "some background separation". The shots
were cells in the Terminal theme, and the kit already tinted the diff and
blended the bar, but only where the theme colours both roles. The
Terminal theme colours none, so the maintainer saw the fallback: flat
bars, changed lines in their role's colour, changed words reversed.

**The terminal says its colours.** A program asks once, at the start
(`cells.TerminalQuery`): OSC 10 and 11 for the text and the background,
OSC 4 for the floor's six (1, 2, 3, 6, 8, 12). The answers go into
`theme.Terminal` (`Hear` for a raw reply, `Hex` for a colour), and the
`Terminal` into the theme the cells paint with, as `theme.Theme.Term`.
Under Bubble Tea, `storybook.TerminalColours(msg, &term)` takes
`ForegroundColorMsg`, `BackgroundColorMsg` and the OSC 4 replies, which
reach Update as ultraviolet's `UnknownOscEvent` (hottytea hands back
what isn't HOTTY's). The Book and `storybook -bare` ask and listen; the
Book keeps the answers apart from the theme picked, so F3 keeps them.

What cells does with them (profile §3.6, "Known colours"):
- A blend (the progress fill) and a tint (a changed line, a marked line
  of code, a field's selection, a surface's fill) take effect where both
  colours are known: the theme's, else the terminal's.
- `selection` is a fifth of the way from the background to the accent,
  and `surface` 24/255 from the background toward the text; the
  terminal's palette has no number for either.
- Everything else is as before. A role the theme leaves to the terminal
  is still written as its ANSI number, so the terminal's own palette
  applies, and no background goes under every cell: only a tinted cell
  has one, so a transparent terminal stays transparent elsewhere. A row
  keeps a tinted blank at its end and still leaves out a plain one.
- Nothing waits: the first frame is at the floor, the next one tints. A
  terminal that never answers looks as it did.

**The diff.** A changed line keeps its tokens' colours on its tint (a
sixth); its changed words are tinted about a quarter (68/255, down from a
third: compared side by side, 68 still stands out from the line and
reads softer). Where nothing tints, a changed line keeps its tokens'
colours too (it used to take its role's), its sign says it, and its
changed words are underlined; only under NO_COLOR are they reversed, as
nothing else reads there (`Cell.BackAttr`, `Cell.MonoAttr`, which
replaces `BackFg`).

**Progress.** The fill blends from `info` into `accent` as the profile
says, now under the Terminal theme too. In Catppuccin Mocha that is teal
into blue: it reads as a gradient on a long bar, but it is quiet, two
neighbouring hues, nothing like bubbles' purple into pink. No other pair
of roles does better without saying something it shouldn't: into `error`
gives the purple and pink, and a bar that turns red as it nears the end
in a palette whose red is red. Kept; see the open points.

**VHS answers.** Its terminal (xterm.js in ttyd) answers OSC 10, 11 and
4, `rgb:rrrr/gggg/bbbb` ended by ST, so the reference shots
(`make shot NAME=diff`, `progress`, `code`) show the tints as a user's
terminal would. Looked at: the diff (lines tinted, syntax kept, words a
step stronger), the progress bars (teal into blue on Install), the code
(highlighted lines on a calm blue, error and change lines tinted; the
highlight was a heavy blue at 30%, hence a fifth).

**Checked:** `make check`; tests for the replies (`theme.TestHear`: ST
and BEL, 1 to 4 digits a channel, several numbers in one OSC 4, what it
leaves alone), the query, tints and blends under a learned palette and
none without one, a row's tinted end, the diff's fallbacks with colour
and under NO_COLOR, and `storybook.TerminalColours`.

**Open, for the maintainer:**
- The gradient's roles. info into accent is right by meaning and quiet in
  Catppuccin; a palette whose cyan and blue sit apart shows more. If it
  should be eye candy everywhere, the bar needs a colour that isn't a
  role (the terminal's magenta, OSC 4;5), which the profile doesn't
  allow today. Proposed to keep info into accent.
- The host's diff (kit.css) still tints changed words about a third (22%
  over the line's 16%). A host knows its colours, so nothing there was a
  fallback; bring it to a quarter with KIT-06h if the cells' softer words
  read better.
