# 2026-10-23.6 — KIT-GAP: room between a Column's children

plx's settings page puts a Divider between its settings, because "a
Column has no gap. With dense, rows run together". In cells a setting's
row, its caption and the next setting's row follow one another with no
blank row, as a form's fields do, and the kit sets a child apart only
where it would read as part of what comes before it (profile §3.3).

**`io_neuroplast_hotty.gap`** on a Column, a Row or a List: a whole
number of terminal rows between a Column's children, or of columns
between a Row's, 0 to 8. It takes the place of the kit's own spacing
rather than adding to it, so that both renditions put the same rows
between the same children and 0 packs them tight. In cells a Column's
gap replaces the automatic blank rows (`separators`), between children
that draw rows; a Row's replaces the one column between children, in its
natural width and minimum too. On a host the container gets `k-gap` and
`--k-gap: N`, which kit.css turns into `row-gap` in `--hotty-cell-h` or
`column-gap` in `--hotty-cell-w`, in place of `--k-2` and `--k-3`; a
drop's line sits in the middle of it. A weight's `--k-w` follows it in
the same style.

**Bounded:** 8 at most, a fraction rounded down, anything not a number
none, so that an agent's gap cannot make a surface thousands of rows
tall.

**Story:** hotty/gap, four settings shaped as plx's (a Row of a name and
its control, a caption under it), a row apart with no Dividers, and the
buttons' Row two columns apart.

**Checked:** the gate; hotty/gap in cells at 60×24 and through
`hotty render` at 70×24.

Profile §2 (Row, Column, List), §3.3, §6.36; catalog instruction 37 and
the `gap` property.
