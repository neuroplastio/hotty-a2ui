# 2026-10-10.15 — Round 2 review: labels inline only, the underline under the input

The maintainer's round 2 notes on fields
([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-10-review-round-2.md)): the inline labels
win ("but the width is a bit too much, especially on the plan field"),
and the underline "looks a bit weird in the padded left cell. Let's try
keeping underline only on the input area" (look-invitation), "Underlined
padding feels wrong" (look-task). All in cells (KIT-LOOK).

**Inline only.** `cells.InlineLabels` and storybook's `-labels` are gone:
a one-line text field's, a DateTime's and a select's label is always on
its input's row, a run of them in a Column padded to the widest
(`labelRuns`). A longText and a Choice's options keep a title row above.
A field with no label has no label column. The labels' run now also
counts when the fields' natural widths are measured (a vertical Stack, a
Card, a Form, a Modal, and a Column's height before its children's
widths are asked for), so that in a Column that doesn't stretch them the
inputs still start in one column and none is cut for the padding
(`TestLabelRun`).

**The underline under the input.** The row is: the gutter, the label and
a space, the inset (a blank column), then the input. Only the input is
underlined, from the value's first column to the input's end; the inset
is blank (`TestFieldInput`). The pointer's I-beam covers the input alone
too (`fieldArea.w`).

**Narrower inputs.** An input is a fixed width, as a GUI form's is, and
never grows as the user types:
- a text field's is 32 columns (`textInput`): room for an address such as
  `firstname.lastname@example.com` (30) and the caret after it, at the
  short end of the 30–40 the maintainer named, so that a form's labels
  and inputs fit in about 45 columns;
- a DateTime's is its value's form (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`, 16) or its value,
  whichever is wider, and a column for the caret. The task card's value
  is `2025-12-15T17:00:00Z` (20), wider than the form, and cutting it
  when the field doesn't have the keyboard would hide its seconds and
  zone, so the value counts too;
- a select's is its widest option's label, a space and `▾`: the chevron
  right after the widest, not at the edge. The Plan field is now
  `Free ▾`.

Where the box is narrower the input takes what there is and scrolls; its
minimum is the label, a space, the inset and 3 columns. While a select's
list is open its options go under the input, their labels under the
value's, not under the field's label any more.

**The caret** keeps its shape. In VHS's terminal the bar runs the whole
cell's height, so it crosses the underline under it and pokes a pixel
below it, in `fg` beside an `accent` line. That may be the awkwardness.
A proposal, not done: while a field has the keyboard, set the cursor's
colour to the accent (OSC 12, reset with OSC 112), so that the caret and
the line read as one piece; its height is the terminal's to draw.
