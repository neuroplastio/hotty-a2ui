# 2026-10-17.1 — KIT-CYCLE: a ChoicePicker stepped in place

The maintainer liked the tour's form on hotty.neuroplast.io (web's
internal/tour/kit.go), its Environment `‹ staging ›`, and asked for that
select in the kit and the storybook. Then, through the web agent, which
is rebuilding that form on the kit: "select value should be centered
between arrows".

**How an agent asks for it.** `io_neuroplast_hotty.cycle: true` on a
ChoicePicker that would be a select (one value, shown as checkboxes),
beside `fill` and `resize`; A2UI's `displayStyle` enum is basic's to
keep. On chips or several values it does nothing. Instruction 32 and the
extension's schema say so. `view.Element.Cycle`.

**What it does.**
- Cells: the select's row, its input `‹ label ›`, the label centred, a
  column more after it than before where they differ, as wide as the
  widest option so that the arrows stay put. The arrows are muted, and
  in the accent while it has the keyboard (accent marks only focus,
  §3.6). No underline and no list.
- Keys (`view.Controller.CycleKey`, shared): ← ↑ back, → ↓ Space Enter
  on, round from either end; Home, End; a letter to the next option
  that starts with it. Enter steps it in a HottyForm too, as a host
  clicks a focused button on Enter. Hints `←/→ change`, and `home/end
  first/last` in the full view.
- Clicks: `‹ ` steps back, the rest of its row on (`hit.step`). On a host
  the label clicks the value, which steps on too.
- Host: a field (`k-input k-cycle`) as wide as its widest option, the
  value a `button role=spinbutton` and the Tab stop, centred between
  `keyboard_arrow_left` and `keyboard_arrow_right` chevron buttons out of
  the Tab order, as a Slider's − and +. The value's `data-keys` give the
  program the arrows, Home and End. A click on a chevron may land the
  host's focus on it; Update moves it back to the value.
- Text: a select's line, `Environment: staging`.

`Element.Picked` and `Element.NextByLetter` moved into view, for the
cycle and both renditions' selects.

**Story:** `hotty/choice`, "Choice picker": a deploy form with the four
ways a ChoicePicker draws: the cycle (Environment, autofocused), a select
(Region), chips that pick one (Strategy) and several (Notify), and key
hints.

**Look points for the next round** (not asked yet): the arrows muted
when it does not have the keyboard (the tour's are always in the
accent); no underline in cells, where the select and the text fields
have one; a bordered field on a host, where the tour has none.

**Checked:** the gate; the story in cells in VHS, with and without the
keyboard; on a host with `hotty render` at 1.6x (material dark); its keys
and clicks in tests in view, cells and html (hottytest).
