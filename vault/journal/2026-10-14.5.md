# 2026-10-14.5 — Control+c twice to quit the storybook

The maintainer, trying the edit commands in hottyterm, closed the
storybook with Control+c in the login form's password field: "we
shouldn't close on ctrl+c if there are interactive elements in a program
that use it. I would at least require double ctrl+c".

A password field copies nothing, and a text control with nothing to copy
leaves Control+c to the program, as a host does (SPEC §11), so the
Book took it as its quit. Cells is unchanged; the program decides.

**What changed:**
- The Book (`key`): a Control+c that nothing copied with asks on the
  bar, "Ctrl+C again to quit", and the second in a row quits. Any other
  key, or a click, ends the ask. There is no timer. Control+q, and q
  while no pane has the keyboard, quit at once as before. A host's copy
  command with nothing selected is its key, so it goes the same way.
- The bar says "Ctrl+C copy, twice to quit", or "Ctrl+C twice to quit"
  where the keymap leaves Control+c to the program.
- `-bare`: the same, with the ask under the surfaces, where an error
  goes.
- The profile's copy and edit-command paragraphs say a program with text
  controls doesn't quit on one press.

**Checked:** the gate. TestQuitTwice: in the password field one press
asks and doesn't quit; another key ends the ask, and so does a click; a
second press in a row quits; Control+q quits at once. TestCopyOrQuit,
TestEditCommands and TestBare now need two presses.

**Not changed:** cmd/review quits on one Control+c, its feedback box
included. Offered to the maintainer.
