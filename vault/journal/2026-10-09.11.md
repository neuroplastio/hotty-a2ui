# 2026-10-09.11 — KIT-07c: HottyScrollView

The sixth phase-1 leg: bubbles' viewport, held against a reference with
the story's own content (`make shot NAME=viewport`; the reference reads
the story's log and document, so the two sides can't drift apart).

**The catalog** (profile §6.11, instruction 11):
- `HottyScrollView` has a `child` or `lines` (a list of strings), a
  `height` (10 by default), `follow`, `wrap` and `weight`.
- `hottyScrollTo(id, to)`, `start` or `end`, is a renderer function the
  agent or a Button may call, as `hottyFocus` is. `hotty.Implement` now
  takes a `Renderer` of the three functions.
- A log is lines bound to a list, an index written per line. A2UI's data
  model has no append, and needs none: a write past the end grows the
  list.

**The view** keeps where each box is scrolled (`State.Scroll`, `Left`,
`Tail`). The rendition clamps it as it paints and reports back
(`Controller.Scrolled`), so `follow` works on what the user did, not on
a guess. `ScrollTo` sets it from the agent's side.

**Cells:**
- The box is its content, 2 columns narrower, then a blank column and a
  scrollbar: a `│` track in `border` faint and a `┃` thumb in `muted`,
  `accent` while focused. bubbles' viewport draws no scrollbar. Without
  one, a box with no edge gives no sign that it scrolls.
- Lines are cut at the box and scroll sideways six columns at a time, or
  with `wrap` take the rows they need.
- A child is laid out at the content's width and painted through a clip
  (`canvas.clipTo`). Its hits are clipped too, so a hidden Button takes
  no click and a half-shown one takes it where it shows. The focused
  element is scrolled into sight, found by a dry-run paint.
- Keys are bubbles' viewport's (arrows, j/k, PgUp/PgDn, b/f/Space, u/d,
  g/G, h/l). Vertical keys are taken even at an end, as bubbles' are.
- The wheel scrolls the innermost box under the pointer that can still
  move that way: three rows a notch, Shift for sideways. The storybook
  routes the wheel to the story under the pointer.
- A Column puts a blank row after a scroll view. Without it, the
  "A document" title in the shot read as the log's last line. The title
  above a box stays on the row before it.
- A wrapping view's natural width was its widest character (measured
  wrapped at 0 columns). It is now its widest line unwrapped.

**HTML:**
- A `div tabindex=0` `--k-rows` rows tall (SPEC §8's `--hotty-cell-h`),
  on `--k-tonal`, with a focus ring. Lines are mono rows; a log is
  `role=log`.
- The host scrolls it itself: the storybook places story surfaces with
  `scroll`, so inner `overflow-y: auto` works (SPEC §5.3).
- The diff now sends `append` for children added after the old ones, so
  a line arriving is one small delta, not the box again.
- A program can't set where a host has scrolled. So on a host the box
  starts at its top, follow or not, and `hottyScrollTo` does nothing.
  Recorded on KIT-07h, with the `column-reverse` probe: Blitz draws such
  a box at its end; whether it scrolls is unchecked.

**Text:** all of it, its lines or its child, whatever the height.

**Checked:**
- Tests: view (mapping, `Scrolled`, `ScrollTo`, the call, hints), cells
  (drawing, keys, wheel, follow, sideways, wrap, a child's clip and hits,
  the blank row, natural width), html (markup, the append delta, `check`
  against a fresh document), text.
- `make shot NAME=viewport`, and the storybook headless (cap.sh): follow
  starts at the end, b pages up, → scrolls sideways, d reaches the end,
  `?` shows the viewport's full help.
- `hotty render` of the HTML: the tonal box, the mono lines and the ring.

For the maintainer to judge:
- the scrollbar, which bubbles lacks;
- the blank row after a box, a new spacing rule;
- the host rule: a box at its top and no follow until KIT-07h, or a SPEC
  change that lets a program pin a box to its end.
