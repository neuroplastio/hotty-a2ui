# 2026-10-15.2 — The storybook's Keys picker and -keys are gone

The maintainer asked what the storybook's Keys picker did, thinking the
new keymap made it redundant. It switched the surface keymap of the
stories' fields between SPEC §10.2's default ("Default") and
`hotty.TerminalKeys` over it ("Terminal": Bubble Tea's Control+b, f,
e, w, u, k, p, n, d, Alt+b, f, d). Mod and the host's platform took over
Control or Command; the readline keys on Linux were what was left, and
which keymap a program's fields use is the program's choice, not a
viewer's. "Yes, remove the selector, keep default. Drop -keys flag as
well."

**What changed:**
- pick holds the Rendition and Theme selects and the filter.
- F4 does nothing; F5 is still the tabs.
- The Book has no keymap of its own: no `keys`, `nextKeys` or `keymap`,
  and no `SetKeys` on its renditions.
- `Options.Keys` and `-keys` are gone, and `-bare` takes no keymap.
- The bar's "Ctrl+C copy" reads the default keymap.
- README: F4's row is gone, and Ctrl+C's row says copy, or twice to
  quit.
- The profile no longer names `-keys`.
- The Text field keys story's description no longer names the picker,
  and describes the default keymap.
- TestCopyOrQuit has no `Mod+c=program` case.

web's Book never set `Keys`.

**Checked:** the gate.
