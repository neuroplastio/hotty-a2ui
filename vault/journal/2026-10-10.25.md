# 2026-10-10.25 — KIT-15c: HottyPaginator

The phase-1 leg of KIT-15: bubbles' paginator, dots or `3/10` turned
with ← →. Story `hotty/paginator`: 22 fruit, four a page, under a
`#### Fruit` heading (a List of a template, `- ${name}` by formatString);
then three search results with `58 results` and a paginator of the
agent's twelve pages, set as numbers, whose turns send `search` with the
query and the page; then a hint line.

**The reference.** bubbles' `paginator` (`ref/gaps.go`, `make shot
NAME=paginator`, `ROWS=26`), set as its example sets it: `•` for every
page, the page shown's in 252 and the others in 238, over a slice of the
items (`GetSliceBounds`); and an `Arabic` one right-aligned on the results
bar. bubbles' own default is `•` and `○`; its example and its list draw
two shades of `•`, and HottyList already did, so the kit follows them.
bubbles' paginator draws nothing but the dots: the program slices the
items and puts them above.

**The catalog** (profile §6.26, instruction 26, as the coordinator
assigned: 24 is KIT-16c's Confirm, 25 the timer's). `HottyPaginator`
takes a `child` or `pages` (a `oneOf`), `perPage` (a literal, 10),
`page` (a DynamicNumber, from 1, as `3/10` reads), `displayStyle` (`dots`
or `numbers`, bubbles' `Dots` and `Arabic`), `onChange` and `weight`.
Two shapes, as bubbles serves two:
- **A container.** With a `child`, a List, Column or Row, the view cuts
  the child's items into pages and gives the child the page shown alone.
  This is bubbles' program-side slice moved into the renderer: the agent
  sends the list once and turns cost no round trip; every rendition shows
  the same items; Tab goes through the page's controls alone; and the
  items and the dots line up past the gutter as in bubbles' example.
  Rejected: a paginator that names a list by id (`target`), which would
  need the view to reach across the tree and leaves the dots' place to the
  agent, and a bare paginator only, which needs a round trip a turn and a
  template that can't index a slice (L3).
- **Bare.** With `pages`, the agent holds the pages (a search): a turn
  writes `page`, then `onChange` runs, its context reading the path
  (L2), and the agent answers with the page's data.

**The view** (`view/paginator.go`): Kind `Paginator`, `PageCount`,
`Selected`, `Height` as perPage, and `Paged`, every page's items, for
cells' height and text. `TurnPage` clamps, writes `page` (bound, else the
renderer's state), rebuilds and runs onChange; a turn to the page shown
sends nothing. `PageKey` takes ← and → (h l, PageUp PageDown) and Home
and End, which bubbles leaves out but a Slider has; it takes them at the
ends too. It always takes focus: the box is a Tab stop before its page's
controls, as a HottyScrollView's is. Hints: `←/→ page` short, as
bubbles' example's help, and next, prev, first and last page in full.

**Cells:** a field's gutter, then the child, a blank row, and the dots
(or `3/10` with numbers, or where the dots don't fit), then a blank row
after it (the separator rule), so the next heading doesn't sit on the
dots. Faint `border` dots, with the page shown's in `fg`: under NO_COLOR
the page shown is still the one dot not faint. Focused: the gutter's `┃`
in accent down its rows and the page shown in accent, as a field's.
The body keeps the tallest page's rows, so the dots stay put on a short
last page, where bubbles' rise (seen in the shot of page 6). Each dot is
a click target; a click elsewhere focuses it.
```
Fruit

┃ • Cherry
┃ • Damson
┃ • Elderberry
┃ • Fig
┃
┃ ••••••
```
**HottyList's dots are these** (`pageDots`): its inactive dots are now
faint too, a look change. Shot against bubbles' list, whose inactive dots
are "very subdued", they match it better.

**HTML:** a `div tabindex=0 role=group` (`k-pager`, `aria-label` its
accessibility label, else `Pages`) holding the child, then a `role=status`
row (`~d`, `Page 3 of 10`) of `span.k-dot`s, each `data-on=click` with id
`~d0`…, or `3/10`. A HottyList's dots row is the same markup, without
clicks. Its `data-keys` give the program the arrows, Page Up, Page Down,
Home and End; h and l reach it anyway. A 3px bar at its start is
`--k-focus` while it has the host's focus, the page shown too, as cells'
gutter bar. A page is as tall as its items there. Part ids: all 26
letters were taken, so the dots reuse `d` (a HottyList's dots), with the
page's index.

**Text:** the child with every page's items, then `Page 2 of 6`; that
line alone without a child, as a HottyScrollView is all its content.

**Checked:** `make check`; view, cells, html and text tests; two vectors
