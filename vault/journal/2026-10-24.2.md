# 2026-10-24.2 — A picker's status is one line

The maintainer saw plx's palette in hottyterm with a long status, plx's
stage hint for `config.set`, wrapped over three lines. It drew across the
rule under it and into the first row, since plx sizes the box with the
status as one line.

- **On a host** `k-pick-status` doesn't wrap: `white-space: nowrap`,
  `overflow: hidden`, `text-overflow: ellipsis`, the same for an error's
  and while loading. The whole status is in its `title`.
- **In cells** it was already one line, cut with `…` at the picker's
  edge. TestPickerStatusLong now holds it there at 60 columns, info,
  error and loading, the picker as tall as with a short status.
- **hotty/picker** shows one: Get a setting answers with the setting and
  its help, wider than the palette.

Profile §6.32, the catalog's `status` and TestPickerStatus say it. In
Blitz at 60 columns the old CSS took three lines and the new takes one.
Chromium cuts it with "…". Blitz has no `text-overflow` yet (hotty-blitz
lists it among its gaps), so in hottyterm the line stops at the edge
without one.
