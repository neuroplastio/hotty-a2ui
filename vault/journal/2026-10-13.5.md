# 2026-10-13.5 — G1 and G2 on a host

hotty landed KIT-23's G1 and G2 with the maintainer's go (hotty 155b24a,
§9.1, caps `drop`). `drop` in `data-on` lets an element be `t` without a
press dragging it. A `t` with `drop` and `data-steps` also says where in
it the pointer is (`tx`, `ty`). The kit pins hotty-go 649c60a.

**What the kit marks** (`rendition/html/drag.go`, `drags`):
- an item that moves: `drop`, with `data-steps` `"0 1"` (halves), `"1"`
  in a Row, or `"0 2"` on a tree's node that takes children (before,
  into, after);
- a moving list's box (a HottyList's, a HottyTable's, a HottyTree's, a
  List), so an empty list takes an item;
- every drop target (`accepts`), so the kanban's Ada and Grace do.

**Where it lands.** `stepFrac` turns the host's step into the middle of
what it spans: 0.25 and 0.75 for halves, 0.125, 0.5 and 0.875 for a
folder. The view's halves and thirds take those as cells takes pixels. A
host without `drop` leaves frac at -1, the rule without pixels. Over the
box between items the line stays where it was, so it doesn't flicker in
the gaps.

**Looks**, checked with `hotty render`:
- An empty List keeps a card's height (2.5rem).
- A list box's ring is inset and restates the outline, because the scroll
  view's `outline: none` came later in kit.css and won.
- Grace's ring sits outside her card.

**Tests:** `TestDragHalvesOnHost` (halves; a folder's three steps) and
`TestDropOnlyOnHost` (an empty column; Grace). The earlier tests run on
a host without `drop`.

**Hosts.** Not on the site until npm 0.1.0-next.13, and not in the
packaged hottyterm until 7d38a45 is released; both are the maintainer's.
