# 2026-10-13.4 — Click runs, and words in a Text

The maintainer: "Let's follow up on the double click stuff and
selection. Get the small stuff fixed before moving on".

**A run of clicks acts once** (`view.Controller.ClickItem`, profile
§3.7). A click on an item selects it, or acts on it when it was already
selected. This covers a HottyList's item, a HottyTable's row, a
HottyDiff's hunk, a HottyTree's node and a HottyFilePicker's entry. The
run's second click acts only where its first only selected, and later
clicks do nothing. Before:
- a double click on a folder opened it and closed it again;
- a double click on a selected row ran its action twice;
- a triple click in the file picker acted inside the directory it had
  just opened.

Both renditions now go through ClickItem. Cells passes its own count of
presses; the host passes SPEC §9's `count` (hotty 7eaa22d). The kit pins
hotty-go 1d58e1a for `Event.Count`. The per-kind click helpers in cells
(clickList, clickTable, clickDiff) are gone.

**Words in a Text** (cells, KIT-12c). A double click on a Text's or a
HottyMarkdown's text selects the word under it, as a field's does
(`wordAt`). A triple click selects its paragraph: the rows a soft wrap
made of one line, joined. Either is copied at once, and a drag after it
extends nothing. A one-cell word counts as a selection (`textSel.whole`).
