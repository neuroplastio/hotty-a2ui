# 2026-10-23.14 — hotty/cheatsheet picks a Keymap

web, publishing HottyKeys, saw two things in hotty/cheatsheet's Leader
buttons: they spelled "Ctrl+B", "Ctrl+A" and "Ctrl+Space" in words while
the caps under them drew ⌃+B, so one screen named one key two ways; and
none of them showed which leader was current. A basic Button has no
pressed state, and the kit gives it none.

The story now does what plx's own settings do: a basic ChoicePicker of
chips, "Keymap", with tmux, screen and Emacs. The chip picked is pressed
(`aria-pressed` on a host, `(•)` in cells), and the chips name keymaps, so
only the sheet's caps draw keys. A ChoicePicker sends no action, so the
program reads /keymap before each draw (`Run.Configure`) and, when the
keys it sent are another keymap's, sends that one's after its leader
(Ctrl+B, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+X), one agent message per pick, logged as one. The
whole lines (Mod+Shift+P, ↑/K) stay as they are.

story.TestCheatSheetKeymap checks the program; storybook's
TestCheatSheetKeymapOnHost clicks the screen chip on a test host and sees
it pressed and the sheet's caps change. Checked in Blitz at 70 columns and
in cells.
