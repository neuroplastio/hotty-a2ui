# 2026-10-23.7 — KIT-WIDTH: a field as wide as its value

plx's settings page: "TextField is fixed at 32 cols with no width hint".
A port takes 5 digits and a path 40 characters or more, and in cells
every one-line text field's input is 32 columns, an email address's
room, whatever it takes.

**`io_neuroplast_hotty.width`** on a TextField or a DateTimeInput: the
characters its value shows, 1 to 256, in place of the kit's own (a
one-line text field's 32, a DateTime's value's form). It is the input,
not the field: the label and the gutter are beside it, and the input
still takes less where its box is narrower, its value scrolling or cut
with an ellipsis. In cells it sets `inputWidth`, so the natural width,
the underline and a run's aligned inputs all follow; the minimum is 3
columns of input or the width, whichever is less. A longText starts as
wide as its width, its grip's column among them, and its grip resizes it
from there (`resize.go` starts a drag from the width as drawn). On a host
the input gets `k-cols` and `--k-cols: N`, which kit.css turns into
`width: calc(N * 1ch + padding + border)`, `max-width: 100%`; `ch`,
since the input's font is the kit's, which may not be the terminal's. A
weight stays on the field's wrapper.

Not for a select, whose input is its widest option, nor a Slider. A
field without a width keeps what it had: 32 columns in cells, its whole
field on a host.

**A2UI's TextField needs its `label`**, so the story is a form, not
plx's Row of a name Text and a control: each field's own label, the
labels padded to the widest so the inputs start in one column, and only
their widths differ.

**Bounded:** 256 at most, less than 1 is 1, a fraction rounded down,
anything not a number none.

**Story:** hotty/width: a host's field 24, a port's 5, a socket path's
40, a display name with none (32), a longText 40.

**Checked:** the gate; hotty/width in cells at 64 and 36 columns, the
underlines 24, 5, 40 and 32 wide; through `hotty render` at 70×24, the
inputs 24, 5 and 40 `ch`.

Profile §3.3, §3.5, §6.37; catalog instruction 38 and the `width`
property.
