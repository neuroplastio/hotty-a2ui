# 2026-10-10.4 — The storybook's nav and tabs

The third item of the storybook revamp the maintainer approved on
2026-10-10, after the tree (2026-10-10.2) and HottyIcon (2026-10-10.3).
It also covers the kit extension they approved for it ("Kit extension
approved").

**Nav is a HottyTree.**
- **The stream** is first, when there is one. Then four branches:
  - Components, the kit's stories about one component, by title;
  - Behaviours, the ones that aren't, by title;
  - A2UI examples, folded at the start;
  - Fallbacks.
- **Where a story goes:** its `kind` decides, or its group for a
  fallback. A story without a kind is one of A2UI's examples (stories
  2026-10-10, 64b3ba5).
- **Selection opens.** The tree's `selected` is nav's `/cur`, and
  `Book.settle` opens the story it names. A branch opens nothing.
  `showing` writes `/cur` whenever a story opens some other way, so the
  branch it's in shows open.
- **Enter, or a click on the story shown,** gives the keyboard to the
  story. Its first pane that takes the keyboard gets it at the next View.
- **The filter** is a TextField in pick. `settle` copies it to nav's
  `/q`, which the tree filters by, so it stays in sight while nav
  scrolls.
- **Titles.** A2UI's examples' titles go in sentence case: "Login form
  with validation". A word with a capital inside, such as "ChildList",
  or an acronym keeps its case.
- **Following the selection in cells.** Nav follows the selection while
  it hasn't the keyboard. The tree now reveals its selected row
  (`Rendition.Sight`), and a pane's `follow` names the element whose
  sight it keeps when it has no keyboard.

**Panel heads the tabs** with the story shown, in two lines:
- its icon (host only), its title, and the A2UI names it's about (its
  `component`, or "A2UI example" or "Fallback");
- its description's first sentence, muted.

About shows the whole description. The header is part of panel, so the
host's fit, or the frame's rows in cells, still gives the preview its top.

**Icons on tabs and options.** The extension
`io_neuroplast_hotty.icons` (profile §6.16, instruction 16):
- **On a Tabs,** a list of names, one per tab in order.
- **On a ChoicePicker,** an object from an option's value to its name.
- **On a host,** the icon goes before the title or the label: on the
  tab, in the open list, on a chip or a checkbox, and on the closed
  select, beside the picked value.
- **Cells and text** leave it out, as they do a tree node's icon.

The storybook's tabs are visibility, info, send, data_object and forum.
Its renditions are web, terminal, notes and vertical_split. Its themes
are light_mode or dark_mode by their background's luminance, and contrast
for Terminal's, which the kit can't know. Its keymaps are terminal and
keyboard.

**Pick on a host** is now a column: each select has its name as a caption
on its left, and the filter has a search icon and "Filter". Side by side,
the rendition and the keymap clipped at 30 columns once they had icons
("Termina"). The column is no taller than before. Cells keep their "Theme:
Nord" options and get a `⌕` before the filter.

**Kit changes along the way:**
- A closed branch on a host shows its count, muted (`k-node-count`), as
  cells already did.
- `storybook/icons.txt` has 37 names now: every story's, the branches',
  the tabs' and the pickers'.
- `hotty/shortcut-press` takes `ads_click`: `smart_button`'s Sharp style
  is hidden in the iconify set, which the generator refuses.

**Found on the way, a Blitz defect.** Inline SVG paints `currentColor`
black when the computed `color` is a `color-mix()`, such as `--k-muted`.
That's why inactive tabs' icons are black in hottyterm (the maintainer
saw it).
- **Cause:** fork 0028's `outer_html` writes the colour with
  `to_css_string()`. A `color-mix(in srgb …)` serializes as `color(srgb
  …)`, which usvg can't parse.
- **Fix:** `into_srgb_legacy()`.
- **Status:** handed to the hotty agent with a repro, since hotty-blitz
  had another session's uncommitted work. The kit doesn't work around
  it.
