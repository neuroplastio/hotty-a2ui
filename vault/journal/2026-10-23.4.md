# 2026-10-23.4 — KIT-FILL: a screen as tall as its pane

plx's settings page wanted its sections to take the pane's rows below a
four-row head. The kit had no way to say that, so plx worked out the
pane's rows less the head and sent the HottyScrollView a new `height` on
every resize.

**`io_neuroplast_hotty.stretch` on the root.** A2UI v1.0 lets a `weight`
be set only on a Row's or a Column's child, so the root can't have one,
and `fill` is the Slider's already. `stretch` is A2UI's own word for
taking the room given. True on the root, the root is as tall as the
surface, and the rows its content leaves go to its weighted children,
as any Column's spare rows do, or as its `justify` says. Elsewhere it
does nothing. Opt-in, since a card or a form is as tall as what it
holds: the storybook's panes are not fit, and a root that always filled
would stretch A2UI's own examples.

**Cells.** `Rendition.SetRows(rows)` says how many rows the surface has,
as a pane's. Every frame is at least that tall; an open Modal's panel is
centred in them, as a host centres it in the viewport; and a stretched
root is painted at the pane's rows, or its content's where that is
taller. A weighted HottyScrollView in a Column pages at the rows the
Column gives it, its `height` the least. In a Row a HottyScrollView is
no longer stretched to the Row's height: a host's box of a set height
isn't. To fill a Row's height, put the box in a weighted Column in the
Row. The storybook's cells panes now call `SetRows` with their rows,
except the fit ones (the story picker, the bar on the preview).

**Host.** The root gets the class `k-stretch`, which kit.css grows in
the surface's flex column (`min-height: 100vh`). A weighted box already
grew from its height in a Column (`.k-col > .k-weighted`).

**A bug the render found.** A weight's `style="--k-w: …"` replaced the
element's own style, since 1bc37da (2026-10-08). So a weighted
HottyScrollView lost its `--k-rows`, its height fell back to its
content's, and a host showed it whole. The same went for a weighted
chart, tree, file picker, code block or QR code. The weight now follows
the element's own style.

**Story:** hotty/stretch, "Fill the pane": a settings screen, its
sections scrolling under the head and its buttons and key hints on the
pane's last rows.

**Checked:** the gate; cells at 60×30, 60×16 and with no `SetRows`;
hotty/stretch through `hotty render` at 70×24 and 70×60.

**For the maintainer's look:** hotty/stretch at a few pane heights, in
cells and on a host; the storybook's Modal stories in cells, now
centred in the pane.
