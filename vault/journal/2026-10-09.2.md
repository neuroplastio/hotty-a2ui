# 2026-10-09.2 — KIT-00: the Hotty prefix

The maintainer answered Q-0001: "Hotty prefix sounds good. Phase 1 is a go."
The question moved to [knowledge/catalog-naming.md](../knowledge/catalog-naming.md).

What changed:
- **The hotty catalog.** `Shortcut` is now `HottyShortcut`, `Form` is
  `HottyForm`, `focus` is `hottyFocus` and `blur` is `hottyBlur`. The
  catalog's description and `instructions` give the rule: a name without
  the prefix is basic's.
- **Everywhere the names appear:**
  - the view's mappers (`view/catalogs.go`) and `hotty.Implement`;
  - the stories under `story/stories/hotty/` and their titles;
  - the key vectors and the tests;
  - profile §5 to §6, which also states the naming rule at the top of §6;
  - the README.

What stays the same:
- The catalog id.
- The extension keys.
- HOTTY's own `a=focus` and `a=blur`, and the `focus` and `blur` events.
- The view's kinds (`view.Form`, `view.Shortcut`), which are the
  renderer's.

hotty-demo spells none of these names, so it only needs a version bump when
it next moves.

To see it: run `storybook hotty/form` or `storybook hotty/keys`. They look
the same as before, under the new names.
