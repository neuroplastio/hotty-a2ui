# 2026-10-09.4 — KIT-LOOK: fields as huh draws them

The first visual leg of phase 1. Held against huh's form and bubbles'
textinputs example (`make shot NAME=form`, `make shot NAME=textarea`). It
changes only the cells rendition; the HTML one is phase 2's.

What changed (profile §3.3–§3.5):
- **Fields** are the text fields, DateTime, CheckBox, Choice and Slider.
  - Each has huh's gutter: two columns, `┃ ` in `accent` while it has
    the keyboard.
  - A field with a label of its own has a title row: bold, and `accent`
    while it has the keyboard. Before, it was a `muted` label line.
- **A one-line text field** is `> value`, with the prompt in `muted`
  (`accent` when focused). There is no underlined row any more. A
  longText's rows carry the textarea's `┃` in the gutter.
- **A select** is a title row, then `value ▾`. Its open list puts `> ` on
  the highlighted row. Before, it was `label: value ▸`.
- **A Choice's box options** are one to a row, `> [•] label`, as huh's
  multiselect has them. Chips stay as they were.
- **A CheckBox** is `[•]`, not `[x]`. Its focus shows by the bar and an
  `accent` box, no longer by reverse.
- **Spacing.** A Column puts a blank row between two controls when either
  has a title row, or between a field and a Button. A stack of
  CheckBoxes or of Buttons stays tight. The rule looks through a Row, a
  Column or a HottyForm.
- **Buttons** keep `[ label ]`, which is bubbles' textinputs example's
  own button.
- **The storybook's pickers.** In cells they have no titles. Their
  options carry them instead (`Theme: Nord`), so the three still take a
  row each. On a host they keep their labels.

Seen in `.shots/` (`make shot`, and before/after pairs of
basic/32_advanced-form-validator, basic/07_task-card and
basic/30_live-invitation-builder).

For the maintainer to judge:
- whether the gutter's indent reads well beside Buttons and Text, which
  have none (a form's heading and its button stand two columns left of
  its fields);
- whether a CheckBox at the start of a Row (07_task-card) should lose
  its gutter;
- whether titles should take the accent unfocused, as huh's do. NEIO-4
  keeps `accent` for focus, so they're bold `fg`.
