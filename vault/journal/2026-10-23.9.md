# 2026-10-23.9 — KIT-WIDTH: a number field without spin buttons

web, gating 851043d on the addon host (Chromium, 1280×800): hotty/width's
Port, `width` 5, showed "742" and part of the "0". The input was 5ch
and its padding wide, but Chromium keeps a number input's spin buttons'
room inside it, shown on hover or not, so 5 columns held about three and
a half digits. Host 24, socket 40 and the longText were whole. Seen the
same through headless Chromium at 700px.

**kit.css:** a number TextField (`input[type=number].k-input`) has
`appearance: textfield`, which drops Firefox's, and Chromium's
`::-webkit-inner-spin-button` and `::-webkit-outer-spin-button` have
`-webkit-appearance: none` and no margin, in a rule of their own, since
a host drops a rule with a selector it does not know. Cells and Blitz
draw no spin buttons, so a host's field is the same field now.

**Checked:** the gate; hotty/width through headless Chromium, before and
after (Port whole); through `hotty render`, unchanged.
