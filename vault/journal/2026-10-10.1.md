# 2026-10-10.1 — Icons: Material Symbols on a host (gov R-4)

On a host, an Icon is now a shape and not an emoji. The maintainer picked
the pack in gov R-4: Material Symbols, Sharp, filled. The hotty session's
fix made it possible: hotty-blitz ffc7984 rebuilds an inline svg when its
colour changes (hottyterm 26.10.09-dev.e902d8b and later). Before it, an
icon kept the first colour it was drawn in, through a theme change, a
Button's variant or focus.

**Package `icons`** (imports nothing of the kit's):
- `Basic(name)` is one of the 59 as an `Icon{Box, Path}`.
- `Path(d)` is an svgPath's shape. It's one only when d is path data and
  nothing else (commands, digits, separators), at most 8 KB.
- `Glyphs` and `Glyph` moved here from `view/icons.go`. Cells and text
  draw them as before, and ◇ for an svgPath.

**The data:**
- `make icons` runs `cmd/iconsgen` and writes `icons/basic.go`, 12 KB, of
  which 9.7 KB is paths.
- **Pin:** `icons/REV` holds `@iconify-json/material-symbols` 1.2.93 and
  npm's sha512, which the generator checks. 1.2.93 is the newest release
  over two weeks old (2026-09-22). `make icons VERSION=…` pins another.
- **The rule:** names go by A2UI's rule (snake_case, `play` and `rewind`
  renamed, `favoriteOff` and `starOff` unfilled), plus two of the font's
  other names for a glyph. Google's Sharp codepoints give `payment` as
  `credit_card`'s e8a1 and `phone` as `call`'s f0d4, and Iconify names
  each glyph once.
- **What it accepts:** it refuses any icon that isn't one `currentColor`
  path on a 24 grid, or an alias that flips or rotates.
- **The test:** it checks the 59 against A2UI's enum in third_party, and
  REV against the pin the shapes were made from.
- **Licence:** `icons/COPYING` names the source and its licence,
  Apache-2.0.

**The view:** an Icon's `name` may be `{svgPath}`, literal or bound.
`Element.Path` carries it; it used to be read as a string and drawn as ◇.

**HTML:**
- `span.k-icon role=img`, holding `<svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" width="1em"
  height="1em">` with one `path fill="currentColor"`.
- The kit writes the svg itself, so an svgPath is only ever the `d`
  attribute. TestIcons sends one that tries to close the attribute and
  open a `<script>`: it draws ◇.
- The emoji route is gone (`iconEmoji`).
- **A Button whose label is a Row** (icon and text) drew the icon above
  the text, in Blitz and in Chromium alike. `.k-btn > *` made the Row
  inline-block, so the Text, a block, went to the next line. A stack in a
  Button is now inline-flex.

**Seen:**
- A new story, `fallback/icons`, shows the 59, the A2UI fixture's svgPath
  and two icon Buttons.
- It was rendered with `hotty render` at 1.6x, in Terminal and Material
  light, and in the addon in Chromium: all 59 are crisp, in the text's
  colour, and white on the primary Button.
- The music player's transport buttons and the chat message's header icon
  draw as Material shapes.

**Open:** `HottyIcon` (any Material Symbols name, a stroke width) and
`icons/materialsymbols/` come later (R-4 question 2).
