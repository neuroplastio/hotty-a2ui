# 2026-10-23.13 — KIT-KEYS on hottyterm: caps over their neighbour

plx adopted HottyKeys (plx f5b22107, pin bb18507) and found, on
hottyterm, a Row of [HottyKeys weight 2 | Text weight 3] about 500 px wide
whose `ctrl+b w t` is wider than its two fifths: the third cap drew over
"New tab", and the Text stayed where it was. The profile (§6.39) says each
Row keeps its caps whole and pushes its Text over.

The story hotty/cheatsheet shows it at 40 columns through `hotty render`;
headless Chromium at the same 400 px keeps the caps whole and pushes the
Text, as §6.39 says. So the kit's CSS is right (a weighted child is
`flex: w 1 0%` with `min-width: auto`, and `.k-caps` is `nowrap`), and the
host measures the caps short.

A probe page, each row a weighted `nowrap` item beside a Text, in Blitz:
nowrap text, nowrap spans and an `inline-flex` box of caps hold; a
`nowrap` box of inline-blocks with text between them is measured as
everything after its first box; the same with a character before the
first box holds; inline-blocks with no text at all even wrap onto lines
of their own. Parley at Blitz's pin learns `text_wrap_mode` only from text
clusters and starts at `Wrap`, both in its content-width measurer and in
its line breaker, so a box that starts a line is taken as a break
opportunity. Upstream parley's 26052b9 (2026-10-19), which puts inline
boxes in the text as U+FFFC with their parent's style, fixes it; Blitz's
pin is older.

Reported to hotty (hotty-blitz owns `../parley` and its mods) with the
probe and a proposed patch: seed both modes from the paragraph's root
style, until Blitz's pin passes 26052b9. The kit keeps its caps as they
are: an `inline-flex` `.k-caps` would hold in Blitz today, but it would
be a kit workaround for a host bug, and plx is not blocked (it gives the
keys half the row).

plx doesn't need KIT-MD-TABLE fixed.
