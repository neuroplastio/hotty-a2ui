# 2026-10-14.2 — Undo and redo, in cells

The maintainer, after trying cut and paste in cells in hottyterm: "OK,
it works in cell mode. Let's add support for ctrl+z as well, tracking
history per each input field (how browsers do it)".

**What a page does, and cells now does** (profile §3.7, "Undoing";
rendition/cells/undo.go):
- Each text control has its own history, kept by the rendition by
  element id, apart from its hottyedit.Field: a Field is made anew when
  the pointer moves the caret, and the history must outlive that.
- A run of typing is one step, as in Chrome and Firefox, and so is a run
  of deleting a character at a time in one direction; the run breaks
  when anything moves the caret or changes the selection between keys
  (the step's after must be the next edit's before). A cut, a paste, a
  word or line deletion and a newline are steps of their own.
- Undo restores the value and the selection before the step: an undone
  insertion leaves the caret where it was typed, an undone cut comes back
  selected. Redo restores the one after. An edit drops the redo steps.
- A value that isn't the one the history left (the agent's
  updateDataModel, another control on the same path) drops the history,
  as a script setting `input.value` does in Chrome.
- The control takes the undo and redo keys with nothing to undo or redo,
  as a page's field does; elsewhere they are the program's.
- Keys: `Control+z`, `Meta+z`; `Control+Shift+z`, `Control+y`,
  `Meta+Shift+z`. `SetUndoKeys`, `SetRedoKeys`, the storybook's `-undo`
  and `-redo`. Without the kitty protocol Control+Shift+z arrives as
  Control+z, so `Control+y` is there for redo.
- 100 steps a control.
- Checked in a pty: Tab into Username, `a b ← c`, Control+z (0x1a)
  leaves `ab` with the caret after `a`, Control+y (0x19) brings `c`
  back, Control+z again; End, Shift+Home, Control+x cuts `ab`.

Not done: the host's undo. A browser's iframe input has its own; Blitz's
and hottyterm's fields are hotty's, sent with cut and paste.
