# 2026-10-10.18 — Round 2 review: the caret in the accent

One of the maintainer's round 2 notes
([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-10-review-round-2.md)), in cells
(KIT-02c): "line cursor feels a bit awkward".

The line stays: the default keymap is a GUI field's, and a GUI's muscle
memory comes with a line (9a691ed, profile §3.5); a block is the
caret of a terminal's keymap (`TerminalKeys`). What changed is its colour. The terminal drew the bar in
its own cursor colour, usually the text's, so a thin white bar stood
apart from the field's accent underline. The storybook now sets the
terminal's cursor colour to the accent (OSC 12, Bubble Tea's
`Cursor.Color`) once the accent's colour is known: the theme's, or what
the terminal said (`cells.Known`). The terminal's own comes back when the
program exits. Under NO_COLOR, and before the terminal answers, it is the
terminal's.

If the line still reads as awkward in round 3, what's left to try is its
shape under the GUI keymap: a block, or an underline caret, which would
sit on the field's own underline.
