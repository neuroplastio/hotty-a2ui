# 2026-10-10.20 — Round 4 review: the underline by state, brighter labels

Round 4 ([feedback](../feedback/2026-10-10-review-round-4.md)) picked its
form over round 3's, in VHS and in hottyterm, but the maintainer saw no
right underline among them: "I want an accent color with half contrast
when it's focused and half-contrast gray color for unfocused ones". And:
"text input labels need more contrast. They are too gray". In cells
(KIT-02c):

- A one-line field's underline is `border` halfway to the background, and
  the accent halfway while the field has the keyboard (`fieldLineMix`
  128 on either, `Cell.LineMix`). The grey is `border`, the grey the
  labels were in, so that the quiet line stays quieter than the focused
  one; the text's colour at half would have been brighter than the
  accent at half. Where the terminal hasn't said its colours, each line
  is its colour itself, round 3's look.
- A field's label (`titleStyle`, every field's) is the text's colour
  faded 30% toward the background (`labelFade` 77), no longer `muted`;
  the text's own where the colours are unknown, since the ANSI grey is
  what read as too grey. The accent while it has the keyboard, as before.

Profile §3.4 (the label) and §3.5 (the underline).
