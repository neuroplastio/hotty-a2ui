# 2026-10-23.12 — KIT-KEYS: HottyKeys, keycaps on their own

plx-settings, for the Keys slide of plx's onboarding: a cheat sheet
("ctrl+b v · Split right", "ctrl+b w t · New tab") that follows the
user's keymap choices, in the kit's caps. Caps were drawn only inside a
HottyKeyHints and a HottyPicker row. plx proposed HottyKeys: `keys` a
DynamicString, drawn through Controller.Keycaps, inline, no focus, no
events.

**HottyKeys** (profile §6.39, instruction 40): `keys` in HottyShortcut's
names, a sequence's chords apart by spaces, alternatives by "/". Each
chord is read with the Platform and drawn as a hint's caps are. It is
one line as wide as its caps, which it does not cut. In cells, the caps
of §6.10. On a host, `kbd.k-keys.k-caps`, inline (a Row of it and Texts
wraps as Texts do), `role=img` with the caps' words for its aria-label
(`Ctrl+B V`, `↑ or K`, `Command-Shift-P` on a Mac): glyph caps say
nothing aloud. In text, the keys as the hints write them. No size
variant yet; plx called it a nice-to-have.

**Shared with the hints and the picker:** view.KeysText reads keys a
program wrote (the picker row's private keysText is gone, and the html
picker's loop), and view.CapsText writes caps as words. splitAlts now
knows a "/" that is a sequence's key ("ctrl+b /", "/ x"), where it split
the sequence before; the old cases ("/", "ctrl+/", "+/-", "a//", "↑/k")
read as they did.

**Story hotty/cheatsheet:** seven lines, each a Row of a HottyKeys and a
Text weighted 1:2, under three Leader Buttons. The story's program
(story/cheatsheet.go), as plx would, hears the leader and sends the sheet
its keys again (/keys), which the HottyKeys bind. A first try bound them
with basic's formatString: the kit catalog's schema refuses a basic
function in a kit component's DynamicString, so a program writes them.

Columns line up while the HottyKeys' share holds its caps. At 30 cells
the share is 10 and `Ctrl+A > W > T` is 16: each Row keeps its caps
whole and pushes its Text over, ragged, as a host's flex does too. The
profile and instruction 40 say so: pick weights that hold the longest.

**Checked:** the gate; the story in cells at 60 and 30 columns, before
and after Ctrl+A; through `hotty render` and headless Chromium, the caps
and the column as above; text rendition. Tests: view (KeysText, the
bound keys, splitAlts' new cases), cells (the sheet's rows, styles, a
Mac's labels, the width), html (markup, role, labels on Linux and a Mac,
k-wrap), text, catalog, the story's leader.
