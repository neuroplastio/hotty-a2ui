# 2026-10-27.2 — An action after a ChoicePicker's pick

The third of the plx tool author's reports (journal 2026-10-27.1): a
ChoicePicker's pick only writes the data model, so a menu of them needs
an Apply button. They built each menu as a Modal of Buttons instead, and
closed it by emptying its one-item template for a frame.

- **`io_neuroplast_hotty.onChange`** on a ChoicePicker, an Action, as
  suggestions' onInput is. `SetValue` and `Activate` on an option run it
  after the pick is written and the view rebuilt, when the value changed,
  so a pick of the option already picked runs nothing. It runs in the
  picker's scope, so a template's context reads the item's pick and its
  id. Every way of picking goes through those two: a select, a checkbox
  or chip, a cycle's step, a host's control, cells' keys and clicks.
- **No menu component yet.** They asked for onChange or a menu, a Button
  whose actions open under it. A ChoicePicker with onChange shows its
  value, which a "Move to project" menu wants anyway. A menu that shows
  none would be a component of its own, and waits for a use this doesn't
  cover.
- **hotty/choice**: Region's pick toasts "Region changed: the next deploy
  goes there", seen in cells.

TestChoiceChange covers a select, a repeat pick, checkboxes of several,
one without onChange, and a template's second item.
