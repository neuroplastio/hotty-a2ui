# 2026-10-24.1 — HottyPicker draws plx's palette

plx builds its palette on HottyPicker and listed six things the kit at
bb18507 couldn't show. All six are in, with hotty/picker showing each.

- **Group letters.** A row's `groupMatch`, [from,to) character pairs in
  its group, as `match` is in its title, for an agent that matches
  `Group: Title` as one string. Lit as the title's are, `: ` still muted.
- **A note in the footer.** A row's `footer`, muted at the footer's left
  while the row is selected: plx's command id (`pane.split`), as Zed's.
  It isn't an action, and it doesn't take a key.
- **A row icon.** A row's `icon`: a HottyIcon or basic Icon name, drawn as
  an Icon beside a label draws it, or a character of the agent's own, such
  as a pane's emoji, drawn as it is. Where any row has one, the others get
  a blank as wide, and descriptions line up under the names, past the
  icons (cells pads them; a host's rows are `k-pick-icons`).
- **What the footer keeps.** Enter's action is kept first, then Actions
  and the panel key, then the note (cut to what's left, gone under six
  columns), then the other actions, the most significant first. At 60
  columns a command row reads `pane.split … Actions Ctrl+K   Run Enter`.
  It used to drop from its left end, so Actions went first. On a host,
  `k-pick-more` holds the others and has only the room the rest leave
  (a basis of 0), wrapping its least significant out of sight.
- **Panel keys.** These already showed. plx couldn't reproduce the gap and
  folded it into the footer ask. The panel lists every action with its
  own key. Its footer is the note, Back Esc and Run Enter.
- **The wheel in cells.** It now scrolls a picker's rows, as it does a
  tree's, a table's and a file picker's (`St.Revealed`, so the selection
  doesn't pull the rows back).

view.PickFooter is the footer's one model, and cells and the host draw
from it. The text rendition shows no icon and no note. Catalog row props
`icon`, `groupMatch` and `footer`, instruction 33 and profile §6.32 say
all of it. story.TestPickerRows checks that the story's rows are
PickerRows' own. Checked in cells at 60 and 30 columns, in Blitz at 60 and
100, and in Chromium at three widths.
