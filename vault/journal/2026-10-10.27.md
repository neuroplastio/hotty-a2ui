# 2026-10-10.27 — A tree with no branches has no fold column

From the docs agent (2026-10-10): the docs' terminal modes are laid out as
the web page is, each nav group a caption Text over a flat HottyTree of
its pages. A tree whose nodes are all leaves still kept a root leaf's
fold column, so the pages sat 4 cells right of their caption in cells
(about 30px on a host), where the page has them flush.

A tree with no branches, not even one with nothing in it yet
(`view.Element.Flat`), draws no fold column now: in cells and text a
root leaf's two blank columns go, in HTML the `k-node-fold` span. The
selection's two columns stay (cells' `│ ` bar, a host's 3px border and
padding), so the pages are 2 cells in, as a HottyList's items are. A
tree with any branch is unchanged.

Its smaller note, a caption tight against the group above it, is left as
the kit's spacing; a docs layout can put a blank row there itself.

Tests: cells `TestTreeFlat`, html `TestTreeScrollsOnHost`.
