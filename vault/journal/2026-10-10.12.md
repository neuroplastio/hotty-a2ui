# 2026-10-10.12 — KIT-LOOK round 2: fields

The fields leg of the maintainer's round 1 review
(`vault/feedback/2026-10-10-review-round-1.md`: form, look-invitation,
look-task, look-validator). They asked for the underline round 1 took
away ("we should adopt it"), the gutter kept with it ("gutter + underline
+ blinking line cursor"), a cell of padding before a value or labels on
the value's row, a select that reads as the text fields do, a focus that
"clearly says which input is selected", and a location picker drawn as a
select or as a list of options. It changes only the cells rendition; the
HTML one keeps its look.

What changed (profile §3.3–§3.5):
- **The value row.** A one-line text field's or a DateTime's value row is
  underlined across the field (SGR 4), the line in `border` and in
  `accent` while it has the keyboard (SGR 58, a drag's line's colour: the
  theme's, or the 256-colour index at the ANSI floor). Its text is inset a
  column from either end. The `> ` prompt is gone: the underline says
  where to type. The gutter's bar stays, and so does the line cursor. A
  longText is as it was.
- **Labels** are `muted`, in `accent` while the field has the keyboard,
  as a GUI form's label over its input; no longer bold. Two layouts, by
  `-labels above|inline` (`cells.InlineLabels`), above by default:
  - above: the label on a row over the value, as before;
  - inline: a one-line text field's, a DateTime's or a select's label on
    its value row, a space before the underline; in a run of such fields
    next to each other in a Column, the labels are padded to the widest,
    so that the values line up (`layout.labelRuns`). A label too wide for
    the field is cut to leave a space and 3 columns of value row. Option
    lists keep their label above.
- **A select** is drawn as a one-line text field: the picked label inset
  a column on an underlined row, `▾` in `muted` at its right end (`…` in
  `muted` while nothing is picked). Its open list is as it was.
- **A CheckBox with the keyboard** has its box reversed in `accent`, as
  well as the bar: the bar alone said which field, not which box.
- **Chips** are an option list now, a row each: `( )` and `(•)` where one
  is picked, `[ ]` and `[•]` where several are, after `> ` in `accent` for
  the option with the keyboard (huh's multiselect). The pills wrapped
  across the field read as neither a form nor a list.
- **Spacing** (separator):
  - no blank row between two fields: a label and an underline part one
    from the next, and "underline also allows to save vertical space";
  - a blank row between a field and any other control (a Button, a bar),
    as bubbles sets a form's button apart;
  - between two controls that aren't fields, a blank row when either has
    a title row, as before;
  - a blank row before a control after a Text whose last block is a
    heading: a form's title ("Sign up"), and progress's "Progress", which
    the review missed;
  - none after other Text, so A2UI's task card keeps its rows together.
- **Widths.** A one-line field's natural width is the gutter and 22 (20
  and the inset either side), or the label if wider; inline, the label, a
  space and 22. A select's is its inset, its widest option, a space and
  `▾`. Chips are as wide as their widest row.

**Open, for the maintainer:**
- Above or inline. Once picked, `-labels` and `cells.InlineLabels` go,
  and §3.5 keeps the one picked.
- The task card's subtext (look-task: "gray tone to the second line with
  a padding"). A2UI's example gives that Text `body`, as it gives the
  title; only `Backend` is a `caption`. Not this leg: either the kit draws
  a Text after a card's first row as a caption, or the gap belongs to
  A2UI's example. Proposed: leave the example's variants alone.
- Underlines run the field's width, and a field stretches across its
  Column: at 80 columns the validator's run nearly the whole card.
  Proposed to keep, as a GUI form stretches its inputs; a cap would be
  the field's natural width.
- The gutter still sets fields two columns right of Text and Buttons
  (the task card's `Due` too), as in round 1.
- The HTML rendition's fields (underline, inline labels, chips as
  options) wait for the h-leg, phase 2.
