# 2026-10-10.13 — KIT-LOOK round 2: suggestions as an overlay

The suggestions leg of the maintainer's round 1 review: "make sure that
this dropdown doesn't affect the layout and rendered as an overlay (may
need borders). I think we should support both styles". Cells only, as
2026-10-10.12.

**An overlay** (profile §3.5). The list of suggestions no longer takes
rows in the field: it drops down over what is under the field, as a GUI's
menu does, and as an open Modal's panel and a toast are drawn, so that
nothing moves as it opens and shuts.
- Draw paints it after the surface and an open Modal, before a drag's
  line and the toasts. It hides what it covers, and its hits go last, so
  it takes the clicks there: a row picks its suggestion, the border lands
  on the list and leaves the field as it is. The frame grows to hold a
  list that runs past it.
- It is a box with rounded corners in `border`, from the row under the
  value row, a column of padding inside, so that its text stands in the
  value's column. It is as wide as its widest row needs, at most the
  frame, moved left to fit.
- A row a suggestion, what was typed plain and its rest in `muted`; the
  highlighted one reversed in `accent` across the box. Past five,
  `1–5 of 12` in `muted` on a row inside the box. The gutter's bar stops
  at the value row.

**Both styles** (profile §6.22). `list`, a DynamicBoolean in the
suggestions extension, true by default: false shows the ghost alone, as
bubbles' text input and fish have it, for a field typed through rather
than picked from, or where a list would cover what the user needs to see.
The keys don't change: ↓ and ↑ move through the suggestions and the
ghost follows the highlight, Tab and → take it, Enter picks it. The view
carries it as `Element.GhostOnly`. The catalog's schema has `list` (its
`additionalProperties` is false) and instruction 22 says when to set it.
In `hotty/suggest`, Country has `list: false`; Command and City keep the
list.

Seen in VHS at 80×24: the suggest story's Command list over the Country
and City fields, labels above and inline, and Country with S typed and ↓,
the ghost `lovakia` and no list.

Checked: `make check`; TestSuggestDraws (the box over the Note field, the
roles, the caret on the ghost, the count row), TestSuggestClick (a row
picks, the border doesn't reach the field under it), TestSuggestGhostOnly.

To see it: `storybook -bare hotty/suggest`; Tab to Country and type S.
