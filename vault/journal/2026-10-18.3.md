# 2026-10-18.3 — tone: a Text's or an Icon's colour, by its role

plexos asked for it: the agents tool in plx is redone as herdr's sidebar
is, a row an agent, each coloured by its state (working, idle, blocked),
and the maintainer chose A2UI with a tone in the kit over plx's own cells
(2026-10-18). This is the gap plexos' vault calls G2: the cells renderer
had the roles, and nothing on a component could pick one.

What changed:
- **`io_neuroplast_hotty.tone`** on a Text, an Icon or a HottyIcon:
  `muted`, `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `error` or `info`; anything else
  is ignored. A DynamicString: a literal, or a path, which in a template is
  the item's, so a list colours each row by its own data.
  - `view`: `Element.Tone`, read by `Builder.tone` in the node's scope;
    `view.Tones` lists the names.
  - cells: the Text's base role (a caption's `muted` gives way to it;
    inline code stays `muted`), and the Icon's glyph's.
  - html: `k-tone-<role>` beside the component's class; kit.css colours
    each with its role, `accent` as the host's accent (`--k-link`).
  - text: nothing. A tone is colour alone, so the words or the icon say
    the same (§3.6).
- The catalog: `tone` in `$defs.io_neuroplast_hotty`, and instruction 34.
- `docs/profile.md` §6.33.
- Tests: `rendition/cells/tone_test.go` (a literal, a template's path, a
  caption, an Icon, a value that names nothing, a data change) and
  `rendition/html`'s `TestTone`.

Not done: a story. The storybook has no status list yet; plexos' agents
tool is the first use, and a story can follow if the tone is kept.
