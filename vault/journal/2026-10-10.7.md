# 2026-10-10.7 — KIT-SLIDE: a Slider that fills from its end, and HottyRangeSlider

A phase-1 leg the maintainer approved (2026-10-10): a Slider whose active
part runs from its knob to its end, a slider with two knobs for a range,
and a story of their own. The rail stays as it is (`━━━━■⎯⎯⎯⎯`). Story
`hotty/slider`, under Components: Brightness (a plain slider), Speed
(stepped, quarters from 0.5 to 2), Minimum rating (filled from its end),
Price (a range, 0 to 100 in fives) and Volume (no label, an icon either
side, in a Row). Progress keeps its one slider.

**The reference.** Bubble Tea, bubbles, lipgloss and huh have no slider;
OpenTUI's Slider has one value. The range follows the web's: WAI-ARIA's
multi-thumb slider (each knob a `role=slider` whose own range ends at
the other knob) and Material 3's range slider (two knobs, the range
between them filled, each knob a focus stop).

**Fill** (profile §6.16, instruction 20):
`metadata.extensions.io_neuroplast_hotty.fill` on a basic Slider,
`"start"` (the default) or `"end"`. With `"end"` the active part runs
from the knob to max, for a value whose chosen part lies above it. Cells
swaps the track's two sides, `⎯⎯⎯⎯■━━━━`, the glyphs unchanged; a host
puts the fill on the other side of the knob. Value, keys and clicks are a
Slider's. Other renderers ignore it.

**The catalog** (profile §6.20, instruction 20): `HottyRangeSlider` has
`label`, `start` and `end` (DynamicNumbers, each bound to a path of its
own), `min` (0), `max`, `steps`, `checks`, `disabled` and `weight`;
`start`, `end` and `max` are required. It is `steps`, a count of
divisions, not `step`: the basic Slider's name and meaning, so an agent
writes both sliders alike. An end with no value is the range's own end;
each is clamped to the range, and the end to the start. A literal end
moves in the renderer's state, as an unbound Slider's value does.
`disabled` and the checks' conditions are literals or paths (L1).

**The view** (`view/rangeslider.go`): Kind `RangeSlider`, whose two
children are `Knob`s (`price/knob/0`, `price/knob/1`), each a Tab stop
unless disabled and named `Price, start` and `Price, end` (`Start` and
`End` without a label). The arrows, Home and End step the knob with the
keyboard (`StepSlider`), stopped where it meets the other. `PressKnob`
is a press on the track: the nearer knob goes there and takes the
keyboard (the start's, midway); where the knobs meet it moves neither,
and `DragKnob`'s first move picks the knob it goes towards, so a range
shut to one value opens either way.

**Cells:** a Slider's row with two knobs, at 60 columns:
- `  Price ⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯■━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━■⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯ 20–70`, the
  rest `⎯` in `border` and the range `━` plain;
- focused: the field's `┃` bar, the label, the range and the knob with
  the keyboard in `accent`, and that knob's number in the value in
  `accent` and bold (`20`–70), so that without colour the value says
  which knob the keys move;
- meeting: `⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯■■⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯`, the end's knob a column right (the start's a
  column left at max), so both show and each takes a click;
- disabled: muted and faint throughout;
- reversed Slider: `  Minimum rating ⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯■━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 3`.
A click on the track moves the nearer knob and drags it while the button
stays down; a click on a knob gives it the keyboard where it is, and
drags it. Its width is a Slider's with a range's value (`20–70`, twice a
value's width and 1).

**HTML:** the field's label, then a track (`div role=group`, named by the
label) with the rail, the fill between the knobs, a Slider's notches and
two knobs, each `button role=slider` with `aria-valuenow` and its own
range up to the other knob; then an `output` as wide as the widest
range. No `−` and `+`: which knob would they step? The knobs are discs
in the accent; the one with the host's focus has a 2px outline of
`--k-focus` 2px out. A spread box-shadow ring drew with square corners
in Blitz, an outline draws round. A disabled one's field is faint
(`k-off`), its label and value too.

**Drags on a host** (SPEC §9.1). With `steps`, the track is the drag
target and reports the step under the pointer, as a Slider's; the knobs
take no `data-on`, so a press on a knob is a press on the track there,
which picks that knob. The track takes no focus, so the host sends
`blur` right after the press's `dragstart`: the rendition lets that blur
pass, the knob keeps the keyboard, and the next update gives the host it
back with `a=focus`. Without `steps`, each knob and each notch is a drag
target, and a drag from a knob moves it to the notch under the pointer.
A tap on a notch (`click`) moves the nearer knob.

**A fix on the way, for Slider too:** the click a drag ends with was
swallowed by the next click on the slider whenever none followed the
dragend, as when the drag was let go off the track. Now the rendition
marks only a drag let go on the slider, and forgets it at any event
other than the click right after (`releasedOn`): a tap after a drag is
a tap.

**Text:** `Price: 20–70`, `(disabled)` after a disabled one, its error
under it. (A Slider's is `Label: 3 (1–5)`, with its bounds; the range's
leaves them out, as asked.)

**Vectors** (`vectors/keys.yaml`), in both renditions: Tab through the
two knobs and on, the arrows and Home and End stopping where the knobs
meet; a tap moving the nearer knob, a click on a knob only focusing it,
and a tap on the reversed slider; a drag past the other knob, a drag
from where the knobs meet both ways, and a press and release on the
track. The player has new steps: `tap`, `press`, `move` and `release`,
each `{on, at}` with a value. Cells finds the cell for the value
(`cells.Rendition.TrackCell`); the host the notch and the step
(`html.Rendition.TrackStep`), and its host now reports steps, as
hottyterm does.

**Checked:** `make check`; the vectors in both renditions; the story and
a sheet of states (the end focused, meeting, the whole range, disabled,
reversed) by `hotty render` at 1.6x in terminal dark and light and
Material dark and light, focus faked with a style, since `hotty render`
focuses nothing; the cells rows printed with their roles, focused and
not.

**Open, for the maintainer:**
- The focused knob's number is bold in the value (NEIO-4: focus shown
  without colour). If that reads as noise, the `┃` bar alone says the
  range has the keyboard, and the knob's accent says which.
- On a host the range has no `−` and `+`, so its track starts and ends
  further out than a Slider's beside it. The proposal: leave it, since
  stepping buttons have no knob to step; or pad the track by a button's
  width each side to line up with Sliders.
- On a host, where the knobs meet one disc hides the other; the end's
  takes the keyboard and the first move picks. Cells shows both.
- The brief named the prop `step`; it is `steps`, the basic Slider's
  count of divisions, so that the two sliders read alike to an agent.
  Proposed to keep, since a value per step would be a second way to say
  the same thing.

A knob is 1rem, as a Slider's, smaller than a finger; the track around
it is 2.75rem tall, and a press anywhere on it picks the nearer knob, so
the touch target is the whole track.
