# 2026-10-23.3 — KIT-MUTED: muted text a host can read

plx's settings page: muted text was #797F8A on its #282C34, 3.4:1, and
2.8:1 on the tonal fill behind a field. Captions, labels and hints are
muted, so most of a form read below 4.5:1.

**Muted is now 78% of the foreground over the background** (kit.css,
was 62%). On hottyterm's default palette that is 4.6:1 on the
background and 3.7:1 on the tonal fill, and still 1.4:1 apart from the
text itself, so a label stays quieter than its value. Rendered through
Blitz (hotty/form): the labels and the caption are brighter and still
under the field's own text.

**What a mix can't do.** The shade comes from the palette's own text,
so a palette whose text is dim stays dim: solarized dark, its text at
4.75:1, gives muted 3.4:1. Reaching 4.5:1 there would need the contrast
computed per palette, and Blitz has no `contrast-color()` or relative
colours; a pane that cares names a theme, whose Muted is its own
(rendition/theme).

**Cells don't change.** Muted there is ANSI 90, bright black, which the
terminal's palette decides, as it decides every other colour.

**Checked:** the gate; hotty/form through `hotty render` at 62% and 78%.

**For the maintainer's look:** the muted shade on the site.
